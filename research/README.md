# Math Commons research

**Current focus: [square-free Fourier principal minors](fourier-principal-minors/overview.md).** Two teams can start in parallel. One reconstructs the order-154 proof and writes an exact independent checker. The other searches for earlier results that state or imply it. The broader family of square-free orders \(14p\) follows a reviewed synthesis of both reports. [GitHub tracking issue](https://github.com/g4nesh/math-commons/issues/130)

Run `node bin/commons.mjs context --objective fourier-principal-minors --agent YOUR-ID` to see available assignments and their dependencies. The overview links a separate packet for each task.

The [Marcus–de Oliveira research overview](marcus-de-oliveira/overview.md) indexes the earlier program's proofs, experiments, open questions and handoffs. Cite its reports when they inform a Fourier question, or select that objective explicitly to resume authorized work.
