**Three pairs in dimension six.** For three eigenvalues each of multiplicity two, forgetting the eigenvalue labels gives perfect-matching marginals for every relative unitary. On the relative boundary, the labels can always be restored, giving one distribution that works for all complex eigenvalues of both matrices. Color symmetry gives interior neighborhoods, and a residual matching test also certifies some nonsymmetric unitaries. Four fixtures each replay all 358 grouped coefficients. The remaining question is whether all interior points coming from unitaries admit the required joint labeling. [Proof, tests and remaining condition](../paired-matching-boundary/README.md)

**Five remaining triple correlations.** For an arbitrary relative unitary in the $(2,2,2)$ pattern, the 45 colored-edge probabilities together with five alternating triple correlations determine all 358 grouped coefficients. The edge data have rank 38; the full data have rank 43. An explicit signed correction preserves every colored edge, and under cyclic color symmetry the remaining positivity question becomes a necessary-and-sufficient test on 15 real orientation variables. Positivity for an arbitrary relative unitary remains open. [Five-moment reduction and exact replay](../five-alternating-triples/README.md)

**All complex qubit Fourier bases.** For $U=(W_0\oplus W_1\oplus W_2)(F_3\otimes I_2)$ with arbitrary complex unitary $2\times2$ blocks, one common coloring law now works for every opposing complex spectrum and every choice of the three paired eigenvalues. The written Pauli calculation places every physical axis triple in a bounded four-dimensional polytope; exact nonnegative lifts at all 66 vertices reproduce all 358 grouped coefficients. The checker and six tests include three complex unitary fixtures. This covers the whole displayed family, including degenerate axes, while the arbitrary-$U(6)$ paired-spectrum case remains open. Independent-owner review is pending. [Theorem, certificate and replay](../qubit-fourier-orientation/README.md)

**Both spectra paired.** With three double eigenvalues on each side there are 21 different indexed pairing products. Their simultaneous weights reduce to seven types of inequality, three proved directly by Cauchy–Binet. Four universal signs remain open. The five-term investigation expresses the smallest sign in squared third-order minors and identifies a missing decomposability constraint. A separate exact obstruction shows that the 45 elementary inequalities alone cannot certify all pointwise spectral data. Its signed separating array has no known unitary realization. **Two positive cases.** Symplectic relative unitaries and explicit neighborhoods admit weights valid for every paired spectrum. In the other direction, if the six three-coordinate pairing products have a hull with at most three vertices, every relative unitary works. An exact example gives a full complex spectral neighborhood of radius $1/16384$.
