# MDO-MINORS · Which eigenvalue patterns admit common weights?

**Written proof, independent review pending.** Theorem D gives inclusion if either matrix has at most two distinct eigenvalues, or exactly three with at least one simple. Its distribution works across the spectra at fixed relative unitary and pattern. The author flags possible prior-art overlap with Drury (1994). Reviewers should check the coupling and marginal identities before using this lemma. [Theorem D and dependencies](../minors/theorem-d.md)

**Obstruction.** For every multiplicity pattern with four or more distinct values, Proposition E uses Drury's dimension-four example to construct a unitary admitting no single certificate valid identically across the spectral parameters. A certificate for an individual pair may still exist. An exact finite reproduction of the dimension-four common-weights obstruction is available. [Proposition E](../minors/proposition-e.md) · [exact reproduction](../literature/README.md#known-common-weight-obstruction-reproduced)

**Open cases.** The analogous three-value claim without a simple eigenvalue is Conjecture F. The pattern (2,2,2) is a suggested first case. Finite optimizations over dimensions 4–7 produced no outside candidate. For the separate Hermitian four-dimensional route, follow the PMSH investigation.
