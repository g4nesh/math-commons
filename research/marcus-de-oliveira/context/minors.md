# MDO-MINORS · Which repeated spectra admit common weights?

**Both spectra paired, all relative unitaries.** A written proof now gives 21 nonnegative weights summing to one and depending on $U$ alone, valid simultaneously for every $a,b\in\mathbb C^3$ when each entry is repeated twice. The triples may have coincident entries. The complete 189-ray classification reduces the problem to four additional physical signs, all proved by a Grassmann compression: 24 exact leading-minor polynomials have positive coefficients and even exponents. Four complex-frame fixtures check the 720-term convention and all 141 polynomial coefficients. This resolves the two-sided $(2,2,2)\times(2,2,2)$ case; independent-owner mathematical review is pending. It is an exact algebraic proof, separate from the Lean forest results. **Corollary in dimensions at most six.** The written results cover MDO whenever **each** matrix has at most three distinct eigenvalues, at any multiplicities. If either side has at most two values, or three with a singleton, reviewed Theorem D allows an arbitrary opposite spectrum. Otherwise, three positive multiplicities each at least two sum to at most six only as $(2,2,2)$; the new two-sided theorem applies. This corollary inherits the pending independent review of that new step. It does not include $(2,2,2)$ opposite four or more distinct values.

**Breaking all eigenvalue pairs locally.** For a paired spectral center whose product hull has positive area, an open dense set of relative unitaries has neighborhoods allowing all twelve eigenvalues and the relative unitary to move independently. The neighborhoods depend on the center. One exact rational example has radius $1/262144$ for individual complex eigenvalue displacements and the relative-unitary operator norm, with two fully simple perturbed spectra checked directly. This depends on the new paired law and awaits independent mathematical review; it does not cover arbitrary simple spectra globally. **One pair split, a full geometric family.** For multiplicities $(2,2,1,1)$ against $(2,2,2)$, let $E$ be the singleton plane and $B_j$ the three opposite two-dimensional blocks. If $P_E P_{B_j}|_E=p_j I_E$ with $p_j\ge0$ and $\sum p_j=1$, a written exact-polynomial proof gives one law for all seven complex spectral values, for every singleton orientation and every complementary two-plane. The 402 sign checks cover the entire closed proportion simplex. For a fixed strictly positive triple there is also a neighborhood of its whole fiber; at the balanced triple an entrywise radius $1/57480192$ is explicit. Arbitrary nonscalar block projections remain open, and independent-owner review is pending. The isoclinic theorem proves all signs on its stated family, not on every split plane.
