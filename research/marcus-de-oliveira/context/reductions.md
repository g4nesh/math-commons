# MDO-RED · Which reductions and sources can I rely on?

**Reviewed reduction.** Diagonalize the matrices separately and retain their relative unitary. Membership in the closed hull is equivalent to the support bound in **every** direction, including for point and segment hulls. Any counterexample would also admit Gaussian-rational spectra, an exactly unitary rational Cayley construction and a rational separator with positive gap. The density argument supplies no denominator or search-cost bound. The independent review explains the missing invertibility justification for the second Cayley factor. **Source audit.** The accessible Zheng preprint claims dimension four. The audit identified supplementary contact-case proofs and programs that it could not locate. To rely on the result, obtain a versioned package, check coverage, and reproduce the certificates. The Shitov lifting route described in the audit has a Hermitian hypothesis. The replacement enclosure theorem assumes simple connectedness. Before reusing a counterexample, match the region, direction of inclusion and hypotheses.
