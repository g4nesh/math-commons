# MDO-RED · Which reductions and sources can I rely on?

**Reviewed reduction.** Diagonalize the matrices separately and retain their relative unitary. Membership in the closed hull is equivalent to the support bound in **every** direction, including for point and segment hulls. Any counterexample would also admit Gaussian-rational spectra, an exactly unitary rational Cayley construction and a rational separator with positive gap. The density argument supplies no denominator or search-cost bound. The independent review explains the missing invertibility justification for the second Cayley factor. [Derivations](../reductions/README.md) · [exact checkpoint review](../reviews/mdo-reductions.md)

**Dimension-four source audit.** The original literature search could not locate Zheng’s supplement. The follow-up audit pins a public copy, inventories 208 proof documents and reports an exact replay of the finite classification. The assembled proof identifies which of the ninety cases have a first-pass report and which supporting arguments await a second reading. The separate Shitov lifting argument has a Hermitian hypothesis. [Dimension-four audit and coverage](../zheng-n4-audit/README.md) · [original literature report](../literature/README.md) · [source inventory](../literature/sources.json)

**Later dimension-four readings.** A separate owner has replayed all sixteen requested group I–IV cases and ten cases from the first half of group V, reporting “confirmed modulo citations.” The latter replays replace several sampled Hessian checks with exact polynomial identities and identify which universal SO(4) inequalities remain dependencies. They do not close the rest of the contact cases or constitute a complete independent verdict on the dimension-four theorem. Late second passes confirmed the T46 coherent-capacity and T48 allocation-II documents modulo citations. The second-half group-V index tracks eleven more cases and records each verdict as it arrives. **Dimension-five automatic contact sets.** Ishaan's exact reduction identifies automatic sets with faces contained in no non-minor facet of the permutation minor polytope. Its order-four replay gives 82 facets and 124 automatic classes among 214 six-contact classes. At order five, seven non-minor facet orbits and nine tested non-automatic eight-contact sets are certified. An 18-square identity proves all 900 inequalities in orbit A for every complex unitary in dimension five and classifies equality. The other six listed orbits still require separate physical arguments. No completeness or rigid-contact realizability claim follows, and the timed-out integer census is not a proof that all eight-contact sets are non-automatic. [Facet criterion, exact evidence and open questions](../n5-contact-program/strata/README.md) · [Orbit-A inequality and equality cases](../n5-contact-program/strata/physical-a/README.md)

**Known false strengthening.** Two exact examples violate an earlier polygon enclosure while lying inside the original permutation hull. The replacement enclosure theorem assumes simple connectedness. Before reusing a counterexample, match the region, direction of inclusion and hypotheses. [Adversarial report and replay command](../adversarial/README.md) · [exact outputs](../adversarial/polygon-enclosure-results.json)

**Contact reductions.** For a fixed unitary in dimension $n$, Ishaan’s independently reviewed reduction moves any violation to spectra with at least $2n-2$ permutation indices on a separating supporting line. If equal eigenvalues have prescribed groups, a separate reduction stays in that family and counts at least $R+C-2$ different contingency tables. This gives four contact tables among 21 when both six-dimensional spectra are paired. The group values may coincide at the endpoint. [All-dimensional proof](../n5-contact-program/contact-reduction/README.md) · [Independent review and exact checks](../contact-reduction-review/README.md) · [Multiplicity-preserving theorem](../grouped-contact-reduction/README.md)

**Hermitian lifting.** Shitov’s published Theorem 19 extends a Hermitian base case of size $m$ with at most $k$ nonreal eigenvalues on the other side to all dimensions $n\ge m$, for $m\ge k\ge3$. Taking $m=k=4$ reduces the four-nonreal-eigenvalue claim to the four-dimensional Hermitian case. Claude supplied a proof of that base case whose dependencies still await independent review, and a separate proof of the lifting step.
