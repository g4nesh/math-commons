# MDO-FOREST · When do rotation histories produce a certificate?

**Written family proof.** With arbitrary complex spectra, suppose the relative unitary is a product of two-coordinate gates on distinct edges of a forest, each used once, in any order. Independent random transpositions supply a single distribution matching every squared minor. The argument allows left and right monomial factors. Eleven exact forest fixtures in dimensions 1–6 checked 3,332 compound entries. The written proof awaits independent-owner review. [Full proof, conventions and reproduction](../baselines/README.md) · [fixture output](../baselines/results.json)

**Lean verification.** For a forest schedule of unitary Gaussian-rational two-coordinate gates, Lean identifies each actual product minor with its endpoint amplitude at every occupied-subset size. Sampling an independent stay or transposition at each gate gives one finite law on genuine vertex permutations. It is nonnegative, sums to one, and matches the squared minor for every input and output subset. The scalar permutation-to-spectral-product identity and the actual diagonal-update principal-minor expansion are also proved over Gaussian rationals. The actual conjugated-diagonal contraction is proved through retained order two; its higher-order form and the arbitrary-complex interface remain open. [Actual product theorem](../forest-subset-exchange/README.md) · [Shared law and exact hypotheses](../forest-permutation-law/README.md)

**Current matrix-side boundary.** `retainedDiagonalExpansion` derives the actual determinant of a diagonal-plus-arbitrary Gaussian-rational matrix as a sum of principal minors, for any duplicate-free finite coordinate list, including empty/full subsets and singular inputs. `conjugatedMinorTwo` then expands an actual two-coordinate principal minor of $U D_b U^*$ in squared $U$ minors; `forestTwoContraction` substitutes the same forest permutation law. These contraction results hold in arbitrary ambient dimension but only through retained order two, so they do not yet expand the full ambient determinant in higher dimensions. Arbitrary-order entrywise conjugation and diagonal scaling are proved; arbitrary-order adjoint transpose and general Cauchy–Binet remain separate. Both exact statement inventories and source-hash audits run through the existing protected `formal/check.py`. **Cycles and glued blocks.** The original weights give 6121/15625 on the triangle fixture. Six corrected weights recover the exact 11881/15625 and all compound entries. Gluing two already-certified blocks preserves a certificate when their supports meet in at most one coordinate and their certifying permutations act on their own supports. This covers the stated successive gluing schedules. On one four-cycle, an exact separating cover rules out every common squared-minor distribution. Its determinant for the tested spectra still has an ordinary convex certificate. Independent-owner review is pending. **How far can the four-cycle weights travel?** Fix the archived four-cycle and its second spectrum. Three varying permutation weights give a positive convex combination along a real interval of length 41. A second distribution with 12 positive weights works for every complex triple of the first three eigenvalues when the fourth stays at $1-i$. Releasing all four eigenvalues rules out a single uniform distribution. A rational linear functional on seven test diagonals scores every pairing at most zero and scores the determinant $11201152/15625$. Hull membership at each individual spectrum remains a separate question. [Proof and exact replay](../four-cycle-spectral/README.md)

**Moving the fourth eigenvalue.** With the unitary and second spectrum fixed, twelve strictly positive weights cover all complex choices of the first three eigenvalues for every fourth eigenvalue on the closed segment from $1/4-i$ to $3-i$. A separate exact calculation extends the certificate to a closed square of radius $1/1024$ about $1-i$. A six-input separator excludes any weights uniform over all triples in an open square about zero. Each of its six inputs has its own convex weights for every complex fourth eigenvalue. [Proof and replay](../moving-fourth-spectrum/README.md) A later certificate allows the first, third and fourth entries to vary freely together when $1/4\le\Re(a_2)\le3/4$ and $|\Im(a_2)|\le1/16384$. Its eighteen supported masses are each at least $1/40447$. [Exact spectral rectangle](../complex-second-region/README.md)

**Which gate minors can contribute?** For the signed Laplace expansion, a nonzero minor gives a matching of its rows and columns by nonzero entries. On a two-coordinate gate this fixes occupancy outside the gate, and the equal subset sizes allow only a stay or a swap. For a forest in which deleting each indexed edge separates its endpoints, `nonzeroAmplitudesUnique` chains these steps: at most one path between fixed subsets has a nonzero product of gate minors. For one Gaussian-rational unitary block, the [local magnitude proof](../../../formal/ForestGateMagnitude.lean) checks the stay and exchange probabilities and both collision cases for ordered gate minors. Connecting this local table to standard complex matrix products via Cauchy–Binet remains open. [Gate-minor proof](../../../formal/ForestGateSupport.lean) · [subset-path theorem](../../../formal/ForestTrajectory.lean) · [checker](../../../formal/README.md)

**Pointwise block gluing.** An affine complex determinant pencil satisfies MDO for every choice of its free eigenvalue exactly when one set of weights matches both coefficients. This proves one-coordinate gluing for any two pointwise-good relative unitaries. Two flat five-dimensional reflections give an order-nine example whose certificate also tolerates small arbitrary perturbations of its unitary and spectra. A separate two-coordinate reduction uses Schur complements and planar numerical ranges. [Proof and replay](../pointwise-gluing/README.md) · [Two-coordinate reduction](../pointwise-gluing/larger-interface.md)

**Sharp path boundary.** For the fixed four-cycle and fourth eigenvalue $s(1-i)$, a mixture uniform over all three remaining complex eigenvalues exists precisely on $[\alpha,1]$ within the path $0\le s\le1$. Here $1808\alpha^3+9419\alpha^2+8398\alpha-1400=0$ and $0.143<\alpha<0.144$. All 24 columns enter the exclusion proof. [Exact classification](../fourth-boundary/README.md)

**Rank-one phases.** All spectra share a single squared-minor distribution for every rank-one rotation with $|1-z|^2\le3$. Fractional triangle packing gives the necessary and sufficient condition for each individual vector and phase. This criterion concerns simultaneous minor probabilities. [Proof and rational witnesses](../reflection-angles/README.md)

**Lean path product.** Raghav’s merged theorem handles arbitrary subset histories and both endpoint orders. It identifies the squared gate-minor product with the product of local probabilities. The standard complex-matrix Cauchy–Binet connection remains open. [Lean source](../../../formal/ForestPathProbability.lean)
