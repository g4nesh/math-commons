# MDO-FOREST · When do rotation histories produce a certificate?

**Written family proof.** With arbitrary complex spectra, suppose the relative unitary is a product of two-coordinate gates on distinct edges of a forest, each used once, in any order. Independent random transpositions supply a single distribution matching every squared minor. The argument allows left and right monomial factors. Eleven exact forest fixtures in dimensions 1–6 checked 3,332 compound entries. The written proof awaits independent-owner review. [Full proof, conventions and reproduction](../baselines/README.md) · [fixture output](../baselines/results.json)

**Lean verification.** For every length of a Gaussian-rational forest gate product, the actual order-two minor is its canonical endpoint amplitude. With unitary blocks, its squared magnitude equals the endpoint probability. The probabilities sum to one over output pairs. Higher-order determinant alternation is a separate formal result in progress. [Source and assumptions](../forest-pair-history/README.md) · [Higher-order proof](https://github.com/g4nesh/math-commons/pull/57)

**Cycles and glued blocks.** The original weights give 6121/15625 on the triangle fixture. Six corrected weights recover the exact 11881/15625 and all compound entries. Gluing two already-certified blocks preserves a certificate when their supports meet in at most one coordinate and their certifying permutations act on their own supports. This covers the stated successive gluing schedules. On one four-cycle, an exact separating cover rules out every common squared-minor distribution. Its determinant for the tested spectra still has an ordinary convex certificate. Independent-owner review is pending. [Full proof, fixture and replay](../single-cycle/README.md) · [exact four-cycle certificate](../single-cycle/four-cycle-obstruction.json)

**How far can the four-cycle weights travel?** Fix the archived four-cycle and its second spectrum. Three varying permutation weights give a positive convex combination along a real interval of length 41. A second distribution with 12 positive weights works for every complex triple of the first three eigenvalues when the fourth stays at $1-i$. Releasing all four eigenvalues rules out a single uniform distribution. A rational linear functional on seven test diagonals scores every pairing at most zero and scores the determinant $11201152/15625$. Hull membership at each individual spectrum remains a separate question. [Proof and exact replay](../four-cycle-spectral/README.md)

**Moving the fourth eigenvalue.** With the unitary and second spectrum fixed, twelve strictly positive weights cover all complex choices of the first three eigenvalues for every fourth eigenvalue on the closed segment from $1/4-i$ to $3-i$. A separate exact calculation extends the certificate to a closed square of radius $1/1024$ about $1-i$. A six-input separator excludes any weights uniform over all triples in an open square about zero. Each of its six inputs has its own convex weights for every complex fourth eigenvalue. [Proof and replay](../moving-fourth-spectrum/README.md)

**Which gate minors can contribute?** For the signed Laplace expansion, a nonzero minor gives a matching of its rows and columns by nonzero entries. On a two-coordinate gate this fixes occupancy outside the gate, and the equal subset sizes allow only a stay or a swap. For a forest in which deleting each indexed edge separates its endpoints, `nonzeroAmplitudesUnique` chains these steps: at most one path between fixed subsets has a nonzero product of gate minors. For one Gaussian-rational unitary block, the [local magnitude proof](../../../formal/ForestGateMagnitude.lean) checks the stay and exchange probabilities and both collision cases for ordered gate minors. Connecting this local table to standard complex matrix products via Cauchy–Binet remains open. [Gate-minor proof](../../../formal/ForestGateSupport.lean) · [subset-path theorem](../../../formal/ForestTrajectory.lean) · [checker](../../../formal/README.md)

**Pointwise block gluing.** An affine complex determinant pencil satisfies MDO for every choice of its free eigenvalue exactly when one set of weights matches both coefficients. This proves one-coordinate gluing for any two pointwise-good relative unitaries. Two flat five-dimensional reflections give an order-nine example whose certificate also tolerates small arbitrary perturbations of its unitary and spectra. A separate two-coordinate reduction uses Schur complements and planar numerical ranges. [Proof and replay](../pointwise-gluing/README.md) · [Two-coordinate reduction](../pointwise-gluing/larger-interface.md)

**Sharp path boundary.** For the fixed four-cycle and fourth eigenvalue $s(1-i)$, a mixture uniform over all three remaining complex eigenvalues exists precisely on $[\alpha,1]$ within the path $0\le s\le1$. Here $1808\alpha^3+9419\alpha^2+8398\alpha-1400=0$ and $0.143<\alpha<0.144$. All 24 columns enter the exclusion proof. [Exact classification](../fourth-boundary/README.md)

**Rank-one phases.** All spectra share a single squared-minor distribution for every rank-one rotation with $|1-z|^2\le3$. Fractional triangle packing gives the necessary and sufficient condition for each individual vector and phase. This criterion concerns simultaneous minor probabilities. [Proof and rational witnesses](../reflection-angles/README.md)

**Lean path product.** Raghav’s merged theorem handles arbitrary subset histories and both endpoint orders. It identifies the squared gate-minor product with the product of local probabilities. The standard complex-matrix Cauchy–Binet connection remains open. [Lean source](../../../formal/ForestPathProbability.lean)
