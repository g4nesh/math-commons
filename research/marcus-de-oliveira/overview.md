# Marcus–de Oliveira · research overview

**Objective `marcus-de-oliveira` · open.** For all complex normal matrices $A,B$ of size $n$ with eigenvalues $a_i,b_i$, does $\det(A+B)$ belong to the closed planar convex hull of $\prod_i(a_i+b_{\sigma(i)})$ over all permutations $\sigma$? This repository has neither a general proof nor a certified outside pair. [Exact target and boundary cases](README.md#exact-target)

| Choose a question | Current finding and limit |
| --- | --- |
| [MDO-RED · Which reductions hold?](context/reductions.md) | The contact reduction preserves both multiplicity patterns. A violation for a fixed relative unitary reaches a supporting line with at least $R+C-2$ distinct table indices for $R$ left and $C$ right groups. The separate dimension-four audit tracks each remaining case. [Grouped reduction](grouped-contact-reduction/README.md) · [Case audit](zheng-n4-audit/PROOF.md) |
| [MDO-FOREST · When do gate weights work?](context/forest.md) | Lean proves one nonnegative distribution on permutations reproduces every squared minor of a Gaussian-rational forest gate product, at all minor orders. Formalizing the determinant expansion over arbitrary complex matrices remains open. [Common permutation law](forest-permutation-law/README.md) |
| [MDO-MINORS · Which spectra are covered?](context/minors.md) | Theorem D covers three eigenvalues on one side when one is simple. Three double eigenvalues on both sides give a six-dimensional test with four remaining inequality types. Triangular spectral data work for every relative unitary, and symplectic bases give weights for every paired spectrum. [Four inequalities](double-paired-contingencies/README.md) · [Spectral triangle](paired-triangular-spectra/README.md) · [Symplectic bases](quaternionic-paired-spectra/README.md) |
| [MDO-TEST · What was searched and certified?](context/witnesses.md) | All 924 exact stress cases in dimensions 4–7 are inside. About 8,500 optimizer runs in dimensions 5–9 produced no confirmed outside candidate. |
| [MDO-WORK · What needs review or a next step?](context/work.md) | For the archived four-cycle and fixed opposing spectrum, the first, third and fourth eigenvalues vary freely when the second lies in the real interval $[0,1]$, the certified square about zero or the new thin complex rectangle over $[1/4,3/4]$. Ordinary Fourier bases through order eight admit common weights. [Rectangle and evaluator](complex-second-region/README.md) · [Fourier results](fourier-composite-laws/README.md) |
