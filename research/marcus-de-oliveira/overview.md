# Marcus–de Oliveira · research overview

The team's active research priority has moved to [square-free Fourier principal minors](../fourier-principal-minors/overview.md). Use this map for prior results, existing handoffs, or an explicit Marcus–de Oliveira assignment.

**Conjecture status · open. Objective ID `marcus-de-oliveira`.** For all complex normal matrices $A,B$ of size $n$ with eigenvalues $a_i,b_i$, does $\det(A+B)$ belong to the closed planar convex hull of $\prod_i(a_i+b_{\sigma(i)})$ over all permutations $\sigma$? This repository has neither a general proof nor a certified outside pair. [Exact target and boundary cases](README.md#exact-target)

| [MDO-RED · Which reductions hold?](context/reductions.md) | The contact reduction preserves both multiplicity patterns. In dimension five, automatic contact sets reduce to facets of the minor polytope; seven non-minor facet orbits are certified. Orbit A holds throughout $U(5)$ with a classified equality locus. Completeness of the orbit list and rigid-contact realizability remain open. Dimension-four case replays retain their cited dependencies. [Physical orbit A](n5-contact-program/strata/physical-a/README.md) · [Grouped reduction](grouped-contact-reduction/README.md) · [Case audit](zheng-n4-audit/PROOF.md) |
| [MDO-FOREST · When do gate weights work?](context/forest.md) | Lean proves one nonnegative distribution on permutations reproduces every squared minor of a Gaussian-rational forest gate product, at all minor orders. The same law represents the actual determinant of $D_a+U D_b U^*$ for all Gaussian-rational spectra at every subset order. The required Lean check covers this result. Formal transfer to arbitrary complex matrices remains open. [Common permutation law](forest-permutation-law/README.md) · [Matrix-side status](context/forest.md) |
| [MDO-MINORS · Which repeated spectra are covered?](context/minors.md) | A written proof covers both $(2,2,2)$ spectra for every complex $U(6)$ with one law independent of the spectral parameters. Together with reviewed Theorem D, this covers dimensions $n\le6$ when each matrix has at most three distinct eigenvalues: any triple without a singleton must be $(2,2,2)$. The split $(2,2,1,1)$ case has laws on all isoclinic fibers, all aligned singleton bases with independent projection triples, and one nonscalar all-orientation family. Review of these written results is pending; the unrestricted one-sided case remains open. [Universal paired proof](double-paired-five-term/GRASSMANN.md) · [Scope and corollary](context/minors.md) · [Exact spectral edge certificates](paired-spectral-hull-edges/README.md) · [Split-plane families](commuting-split-projections/README.md) |
| [MDO-TEST · What was searched and certified?](context/witnesses.md) | All 924 exact stress cases in dimensions 4–7 are inside. The split-ray search adds 140 numerical runs and 35 nearby exactly unitary positive endpoints; neither this nor the PMSH(5) searches proves a universal sign. |
| [MDO-WORK · What needs review or a next step?](context/work.md) | For fixed spectra with positive-area hulls and exactly two permutation labels per edge, an explicit residual bound gives a neighborhood of the whole common-minor locus for any $n\ge3$. PMSH(5), general split planes and the formal arbitrary-complex interface are distinct open directions.
