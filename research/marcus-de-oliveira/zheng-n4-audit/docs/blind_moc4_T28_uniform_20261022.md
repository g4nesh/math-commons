## Deviations and limits

- G34 and G46 (1376 and 1549 terms) were not recomputed symbolically from a 14 x 14 determinant. They were loaded from `blind_moc4_T28_bad34/46_full_radial_20261022.json` as data. My own C and Q (computed from the lib cubic of paper (A.2)) agree with the JSON exactly. The identity Q^4 det Hess C(g - (C/Q) kappa) = -4 Q^2 G^2 was checked exactly at 10 random integer points per branch, with coordinates uniform in [1, 10^6] and d = 1. The Hessian is computed from my own cubic and its determinant with python-flint. After multiplying by Q^10 both sides have degree <= 50, so a false identity would pass all 10 points with probability < 1e-42. This is a randomized identity test, not a symbolic proof. (Tool note: a first version used sympy nsimplify, which rounded one huge exact rational and produced a spurious mismatch. Removing it fixed this, and plain exact Rationals are now used.) Since every certificate below is about G, this is the one non-symbolic link in the chain.
- My certificate tables are not the document's tables. I cleared denominators my own way, so the term counts differ in places. For example, the F8 bp<1 coefficients have 291/283/166 terms here against the (291, 542, 556) the document states. The b>=1 groupings need 19/30/46 univariate certificates here against the stated 19/24/42. The sign conclusions are the same.
- The old-H, chain and four-coefficient inequalities (3) and the families used in Lemma 2 are only cited by the document ("previously proved", witnesses "in the positive-cone verifier JSON"). No source document is named in the PDF. I did not audit them; I tested their transported forms numerically at the boundary of each.
- The main statement was tested only briefly. I took 4 floating-point points on the T_29 stratum from lib `random_point_on_contact_stratum` with margin 0. They are degenerate, with many extra contacts. Each got 10 BFGS restarts over SO(4). The worst min Phi was -1.2e-10, at the level of the solver's residual (gaps off T down to -2e-10). This was a scratch run and is not in the replay file.

## A. Statements

Theorem 1 (p. 1). Let T = {0,4,5,6,7,8}. This is literally the paper's T_29 (EXACT, lib CONTACT_SETS[29]). Take x in X with P c(x) >= 0 and (P c(x))_pi = 0 for pi in T, where contacts are non-strict and additional contacts and zero coefficients are allowed. Then Phi_{c(x)}(O) >= 0 for all O in SO(4) (O(4) equivalent). No genericity is assumed. The proof is purely algebraic on the full closed cone, using only x in X (x and its conjugate are both singular points of the cubic). So it covers all spectra, including repeated eigenvalues and a_i + b_j = 0. The statement is exactly (*) for T_29.

Lemma 2 (p. 3). Write the cost as (2): g46 = p f4 + r f3 + a f2 + b f33 + c f5 + q f6 + f46 + s f45, all coefficients >= 0. Suppose beta1 + beta2 = 1 and 0 <= delta_i <= beta_i. Then Phi_g(O) >= 0 for all O whenever a >= 1 - delta1 - delta2 + beta1^2/b + beta2^2/c + delta1^2/p + delta2^2/q, with the conventions 0/0 = 0 and x^2/0 = +inf. The lemma does not use spectral realizability.

## D. Falsification

- The transported cited bounds were evaluated at their boundaries: 7 families, 6 random parameter samples each, 12 BFGS restarts per sample (seeds 0-5, rng seed 7). The worst min Phi was -7e-16 (round-off). Every family is tight, with minimum about 0.
- The intermediate positivity claims about G34 and G46 are all coefficient-sign statements, and these were replayed exactly rather than sampled.
- The main statement was tested briefly, as described in Deviations; no violation was found.

## E. Verdict

**confirmed-modulo-citations.** Every displayed identity and every certificate replays exactly. The reduction, the radial-necessity argument (supplied) and the limit arguments are sound. The citations are the old-H, chain and four-coefficient pointwise inequalities. The PDF does not name where they are proved, so they are unresolved CITED dependencies, though they are numerically consistent and tight. The paper's Lemma 4.2 and Theorem 1.2 are also cited. One link is randomized rather than symbolic: the identity Q^4 det H = -4 Q^2 G^2 was checked at 10 random integer points per branch (failure probability < 1e-42).

Presentation notes, not gaps: the table sizes quoted are specific to the author's denominator clearing. The radial necessity is attributed to a previous document but is short and is supplied in step 4.
