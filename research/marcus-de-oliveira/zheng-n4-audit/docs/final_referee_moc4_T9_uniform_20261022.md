## D. Falsification

- SOC (1): minimized Phi_f28 + 2 sqrt(Phi_f1 Phi_f11) over SO(4) with 40 restarts (Nelder-Mead then BFGS, seed 11). Minimum 2.5e-28, so no violation.
- Four-coefficient (2): 25 random (a, B) on the equality boundary with d = 1 (seed 5). Worst min_O Phi was 3e-27. Shrinking c - d by 10% gives min_O Phi = -2.3e-7, so the stated capacity is essentially sharp and the numeric search is sensitive enough to see a violation when one exists.
- Main statement: 12 supporting points in region I and 12 in region II, 8 restarts each. Worst normalized min_O Phi was -8.5e-16, which is 0 up to rounding (the minimum is attained at contact permutation matrices).
