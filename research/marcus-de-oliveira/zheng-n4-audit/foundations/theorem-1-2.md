## Deviations and limits

- I never executed or imported anything from the supplement. `thm12_numeric.py` is my own code, and it reads no supplement file.
- I did not re-read Howard-Millson-Snowden-Vakil (HMSV) or Eisenbud-Popescu (no network). This does not matter, because neither citation is needed (see Section 3).
- Test 2b ("trace the circle B starting from a Haar U") was run **in reverse**. I started from the elliptic side: a real Weierstrass curve with 8 real marked points, then the circle B, then unitaries U_L. This runs every step of the paper's construction, but on unitaries produced by that construction. Going forward from an arbitrary Haar U would need a numerical Weierstrass reduction of the base curve of the pencil of quadrics through [I,U] and elliptic logarithms of the 8 points. I did not implement that. For Haar U I tested something weaker. The optimizer finds O-, O+, lambda (N1). I then checked that interior points of the segment it finds are again unitary points (2 instances). The paper's B-segment has this property, but I did not prove that the optimizer returns that same segment.
- "Positive" numerical results are evidence, not proof. Each tolerance is stated with its result.

## 2. Gaps and doubtful points

No mathematical gap was found. The proof is correct as written up to the expository looseness below, none of which affects validity.

- p.9 and p.11, eq. (2.7)-(2.8): "the nontrivial automorphism of this double cover is iota" is justified "by (2.4)", which in turn uses the Gale invariance attributed to HMSV. The invariance is elementary (step 4), and the deck involution also follows from div q_A = L_A + iota(L_A) alone (step 13).
- p.12: "the resulting solution varies locally continuously with L" is stated without argument. The argument (constant rank 7, nonvanishing entries proved pointwise) is short and is given in step 19.
- p.11: "σ_J(L_0) = iota(L_0)" is stated. It follows by pulling O(1) back through the composite that defines σ_E (step 16).
- p.13-14, Section 2.5: the Zariski openness of "the base curve is smooth" and "exactly a pencil" is asserted. Both are standard.
- p.10: Eisenbud-Popescu Cor. 3.2 is cited as "Goppa duality", but the paper itself gives the residue proof, which is complete for genus 1 (step 8).

**Differences from the earlier write-ups.** `root_unitary_two_orthogonals_20261022` (both copies identical except two sentences of wording on lines 8-10 and 102) has the same proof in compressed form. It lacks the GRR computation details (it states deg V = -1 by "Riemann-Roch / GRR integrates half the square") and gives the D_0 uniqueness in two lines. It also has an extra Section 6, a "sharp obstruction bound" transferred from an external report by S. Holden. That section is not in the paper and I did not audit it. `one_circle.pdf` Section 2 (Proposition 2) uses a different endpoint argument. It writes conj V = D_1 V D_2 with *diagonal* D_1, D_2 and takes "unit-phase square roots". In the chart [I,U] the left factor is indeed forced to be diagonal (it must fix e_1..e_4). The unstated detail |m_i d_j| = 1 holds after normalization on the connected support of U. So that version is also valid, but less complete than the paper's antilinear-involution argument, which is the one I audited.
