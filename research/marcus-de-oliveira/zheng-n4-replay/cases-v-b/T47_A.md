## Statements proved

### Document 1 (assembly)

**Theorem.** If $x\in X$ and $c(x)\in N_T$, $T=\{1,3,5,6,7,8\}$, then $\Phi_{c(x)}(O)\ge0$ for every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$. Non-strict contacts are included (the hypothesis is $c(x)\in N_T$), and so are all spectra (only $x\in X$ is used).

What the note itself establishes, and what it delegates:

1. Every $c\in N_T$ is either a nonnegative combination of the nine minor generators, or has the unique form $g$ above with $d>0$ and $q,p,j,c,e,b,a\ge0$. The coefficients are gaps: $q,p,j,c,e,b,a$ are the values of $c(x)$ at the permutations $18,22,0,20,19,9,14$, and $d$ is the value at 10 minus the value at 20. Proved in the note (EXACT + PROOF-READ here).
2. If one of $q,p,j,c,e,b$ is zero (the others, $a$ included, arbitrary $\ge0$): reduced to the contact cases $T_{29},T_{36},T_1,T_{39},T_{42},T_7$ respectively. The reductions are EXACT here; the cases are CITED.
3. If $a=0$ and $q,p,j,c,e,b>0$: delegated to part C (CITED-INTERNAL).
4. If all eight coefficients are positive, normalize $d=1$. Then $G_9(q,p,j,c,e,b,a)=0$ (EXACT + PROOF-READ here), and $(q,b)$ lies in exactly one of the three sectors $\{q,b\le1\}\cup\{q=b\}$, $\{qb\le1,\ \max(q,b)>1\}$, $\{qb>1,\ q\ne b\}$. Each sector is delegated to one note of part B (CITED-INTERNAL).
5. The capacity (5): $ab(e-1)\ge a+b+e+1$ implies $\Phi_g\ge0$, for all $q,p,j,c\ge0$, $d=1$. This is the cited four-coefficient inequality at the orbit member $(f_4,f_6;f_2;f_{28})$; it is also proved directly here.

### Document 2 (six-ray capacity)

All statements are for $d=1$ and extend to $d>0$ by homogeneity.

- **Theorem 1 (sufficiency).** Let $q,b>0$ and let $\beta,t,v$ be real with $\ell=qb-\beta^2>0$ and $j-t^2>(bt^2-2\beta tv+qv^2)/\ell$ (both strict). Put $s=q+b+2\beta$, $h=1+\beta$, $\Delta=s(j-t^2)-(t+v)^2$; then $\Delta>0$ and $b(j-t^2)-v^2>0$. If
  $$p\ \ge\ p_*=\frac{(j-t^2)h^2-2h(t+v)(1+v)+s(1+v)^2}{\Delta},\qquad c\ \ge\ c_*=\frac{(t-v)^2}{j-t^2-v^2/b}$$
  (both non-strict), then $\Phi\ge0$ on $\mathrm{SO}(4)$ for $q f_{27}+p f_1+j f_{59}+c f_5+f_{28}+b f_6$, and for this cost plus any nonnegative combination of minor vectors (in particular $e f_2+a f_4$). Every limit of such coefficient tuples also has $\Phi\ge0$.
- **Remark verified here.** The certificate is a rational identity. It remains valid wherever $\Delta\ne0$ and $b(j-t^2)\ne v^2$, and the Gram matrix is positive semidefinite whenever the $3\times3$ matrix $\begin{pmatrix}q&\beta&t\\\beta&b&v\\t&v&j-t^2\end{pmatrix}$ is. Corollary 2 uses this with $\ell=0$.
- **Transport.** One element of $G$ maps $(f_{27},f_1,f_{59},f_5,f_{28},f_6)$ to $(f_0,f_{45},f_5,f_9,f_{35},f_7)$, six of the eight generators of the paper's $T_{65}$. In the names of the documents for that cone ($g_{47}=q f_2+p f_9+j f_1+c f_{45}+e f_5+d f_{35}+b f_0+a f_7$): $(q,b,j,p,c)_{46}\mapsto(b,a,e,c,p)_{47}$. The contact set is not mapped to $T_{65}$; only the six rays are transported.
- **Corollary 2 (names of the $T_{65}$ cone, $d_{47}=1$).** Let $b>1$, $0<a<1/b$, $p,e>0$, $r=\sqrt{ab}$, $\kappa=1+1/b$, $\rho=1+r/b$, $\eta=(1-a)/(b-r)$, $k=b(1-r)^2/(b-r)^2$. For any real $t$ with $e>\kappa t^2$: if $c\ge k+(1-\eta t)^2/(e-\kappa t^2)$ and $p\ge\rho^2t^2/(e-\kappa t^2)$, then $\Phi\ge0$ for $b f_0+a f_7+e f_5+c f_{45}+p f_9+f_{35}$ plus minor vectors. In particular $e\le p\kappa^2/(\eta^2(p\kappa+\rho^2))$ together with $c\ge1/e+k-\eta^2/\kappa$ suffices (both non-strict). The first statement was verified for all $a,b>0$ with $a\ne b$.
- **Corollary 3.** Same $a,b$; for all $p,e>0$, with $D_0=\kappa+\rho^2/p$, $M_0=D_0+\eta^2e$, $N=M_0^2-\kappa\eta^2e>0$: $c\ge k+D_0^2/(eN)$ suffices.
- **Corollary 4.** Same $a,b$; for all $p,e>0$, with $A=\eta^2e$: $c\ge k+(D_0-A)^2/(e(D_0^2-\kappa A))$ suffices if $A\le D_0$, and $c\ge k$ suffices if $A\ge D_0$. Consequence stated in the note: a cost outside this capacity with $c>k$ has $\eta^2e<D_0$.
- **Classification (second half of Theorem 1).** Among positive semidefinite $8\times8$ matrices satisfying the ten linear relations of the certificate with $e=a=0$, the weights $(q,p,j,c,1,b)$ that occur are exactly the closure of the family above. Checked in part (see D2-6); no support inequality depends on it.

### Document 3 (five-ray core capacity)

- **Theorem 1 (sufficiency).** Let $q,b>0$ and $\beta,t$ real with $\ell=qb-\beta^2>0$ and $j>(1+(q+b-2\beta)/\ell)\,t^2$ (strict). With $s,h$ as above and $\Delta=sj-(s+4)t^2>0$: if
  $$p\ \ge\ p_0=\frac{(j-t^2)h^2-4th(t-1)+s(t-1)^2}{\Delta}=\frac{h^2}{s}+\frac{(s+kt)^2}{s\,(sj-(s+4)t^2)},\qquad k=2-q-b,$$
  then $\Phi\ge0$ on $\mathrm{SO}(4)$ for $p f_1+j f_{59}+q f_{27}+b f_6+f_{28}$ plus any minor vectors. Limits are included. It is the slice $v=t$ of the six-ray family (after $t\mapsto-t$).
- **Theorem 2.** Classification of the same kind as in document 2 for the five rays. Checked in part; not needed.
- **Corollary 3.** Let $d>0$, $j>0$, $q,b\ge0$ and $(q+b-d)^2\le qb$ (non-strict). If $p\ge d^2/j+2d-q-b$, then $\Phi\ge0$ for $p f_1+j f_{59}+q f_{27}+b f_6+d f_{28}$ plus minor vectors. On the $T_{65}$ cone: if $(a+b-d)^2\le ab$, $e>0$ and $c\ge d^2/e+2d-a-b$, then $\Phi\ge0$ for $c f_{45}+e f_5+a f_7+b f_0+d f_{35}$ plus minor vectors. Verified here directly on the closed condition, the point $q=b=d$ and the points with a vanishing leaf included, without a limit.

### Document 5 (negative Heron factor)

Let $D=a+d$, $M=(c+d)(a+d+e)$, $L=M+bD$, $H_6=[L(p+q)-bD(c+e)]^2-4pqML+4cebDM$.

**Theorem.** Let all eight coefficients be $\ge0$ and $b,j,d>0$. If $x\in X$ and $c(x)=g$, then $H_6(g)\ge0$. It is a necessary condition only.

### Document 4 (both Heron factors)

Let $H_8=W_0^2+4dDb^2L\,\Xi$ with $W_0=(q+d)Lp+b\{(q-d)L-ae(c+d)\}$ and $\Xi=q(c+e+d+b)-ce$.

**Theorem.** Under the same hypotheses, $H_6(g)\ge0$ and $H_8(g)\ge0$. Necessary conditions only.

Both theorems hold already for coefficients $\ge0$ with $b,d>0$; $j>0$ is not needed (step H13).
