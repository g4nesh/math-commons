## Statement and scope

Case $T_{38}$: if $x\in X$ and $c(x)\in N_{T_{38}}$, then $\Phi_{c(x)}(O)\ge0$ for every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.

- **Non-strict contacts.** Only $c(x)\in N_T$ is used. Zero coefficients, hence additional contacts, are allowed everywhere: $p,c,b$ may vanish inside the charts, $d=0$ is a sum of minors, and the faces $q,j,e,a=0$ are treated separately (step 4 and the Observation). PROOF-READ.
- **Every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.** The conclusion is that $c(x)$ is a nonnegative combination of squared-minor vectors and of combinations covered by the cited inequalities, each valid on all of $\mathrm{SO}(4)$.
- **All spectra.** The proof never parametrizes by spectra. It uses only $x\in X$, through $X\subset\{\nabla C=0\}$ (EXACT) and the two equations of PROOF.md 7.3. Repeated eigenvalues and $a_i+b_j=0$ are covered by the same argument; nothing is delegated to Appendix B or Theorem C.1. PROOF-READ.

The logical shape: $N_T$ is a simplicial cone with one non-minor generator $f_{56}$. Four conditions (2)–(5) on the coefficients are each sufficient for $\Phi\ge0$. Where all four fail (the *hard region*), the radial equations force $G_9=0$ for a polynomial $G_9$ of degree 9; seventeen charts cover the hard region, and in each $G_9>0$. So no point of $X$ is hard.

## Observation (not in the document): the four cited cases are not needed

The document removes the faces $q=0$, $j=0$, $e=0$, $a=0$ by citing other cases before it normalizes. Its own certificates already cover them. All of the following is EXACT (section I of the script), with a randomized exact test of the coverage (falsification test 3).

1. **$C_0>0$ on the faces.** The only negative term of $C_0$ is $-jea$: $C_0+jea$ has only positive coefficients and contains $d^3$. So $C_0\ge d^3>0$ whenever $jea=0$, and $Q\ne0$, $G_9=0$ follow as in steps 9, 10.
2. **Face $j=0$.** $G_9$ restricted to $j=0$ has 489 terms, all coefficients nonnegative, and contains $d^9$. So $G_9\ge d^9>0$ there: no point of $X$ with $c(x)\in N_T$ has $j=0<d$, and nothing needs to be proved on this face. ($T_4$ is not needed.)
3. **Faces $q=0$ and $a=0$.** The charts are defined at $Q=0$ and at $A=0$ (no denominator vanishes), and the inverses of step 11 still apply; at $a=0$ condition (4) reads $j\ge1+1/e$. In each of the fifteen direct charts the nonnegative remainder has a positive monomial in the strictly positive parameters other than $Q$ and $A$ (for B1, B2 it is $J^2$), and the bounds of A4, E4 do not involve $Q$ or $A$. So $G_9>0$ on the hard region also for $q=0$ or $a=0$. ($T_{46}$, $T_{11}$ are not needed.)
4. **Face $e=0$.** Then $u_0=0$, condition (5) is unavailable and (4) reads $j\ge1+1/a$ (unavailable if also $a=0$). The five charts with $u_0<1$ are defined at $U=0$, where $e=0$, and their remainders have a positive monomial free of $U$, $Q$ and $A$ ($J^2$, $J^2$, $T^2J^2$, $J^2$, $1$ for A1, B1, C1, D1, E1). So $G_9>0$ on the hard region also for $e=0$, alone or together with $q=0$, $a=0$. ($T_8$ is not needed.)

Consequence: for every $x\in X$ with $c(x)\in N_T$ and $d>0$, one of (2)–(5) holds (with a condition regarded as unavailable when its denominator vanishes). Case $T_{38}$ then rests on C1–C3 only. This removes a dependence of a group V case on $T_8$, a group VI case of stage 4.
