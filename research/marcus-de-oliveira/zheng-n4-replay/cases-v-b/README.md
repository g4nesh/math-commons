# Independent replay of the contact cases of group V, second half (Zheng's proof for n = 4)

This page tracks eleven cases. The verdict table lists completed replays and links to their reports and scripts as they become available.

- **Task:** `mdo-zheng-cases-v-b`, requested by the audit `mdo-zheng-n4-audit` (owner `IshaanAyaan`). Replay by `ganesh-claude-minors` (Claude Code, model `claude-fable-5-2`) and its workers, GitHub owner `g4nesh`. November 26, 2026.
- **Procedure.** As in [the first half of group V](../cases-v-a/README.md), each worker reads the proof documents in the author’s public supplement, replays the identities and certificates with its own exact code, and writes its verdict before opening the audit’s first-pass report. Scripts read the supplement as data from a local clone named by `MOC4_SUPP`. The supplement’s programs remain outside the replay.
- **Group V.** The real part of a point of the matching cone satisfies C = 0 and a Hessian equation. Written in the generators of the contact cone, these exclude, by polynomial sign certificates, the region where every universal inequality on SO(4) fails.
- **Status.** Reports are added as workers finish. A verdict here is one independent reading by a second owner, not a formal verification. The coordinator re-runs every replay script before committing it.
- **Evidence levels and verdict words** are those of [`../../zheng-n4-audit/METHOD.md`](../../zheng-n4-audit/METHOD.md).

## T₄₇: replayed in parts, no verdict yet

The proof of T₄₇ = {1,3,5,6,7,8} is an assembly of eleven notes; that of T₆₅ = {0,4,5,6,9,12} has 22. They are replayed in parts: every document belongs to exactly one part, each part states the exact region and conclusion of every note it replays, and the coordinator checks that the parts fit together.

**Part A of T₄₇** ([T47_A.md](T47_A.md)) covers five notes: the top-level note, two notes with sufficient conditions and two with necessary inequalities. Its verdict is confirmed modulo citations, with no gap. Scripts: [`replay_T47_A_assembly.py`](replay_T47_A_assembly.py) (58 exact and 4 numeric checks), [`replay_T47_A_capacities.py`](replay_T47_A_capacities.py) (47 and 2), [`replay_T47_A_heron.py`](replay_T47_A_heron.py) (28 and 4), with the helper [`replay_T47_A_common.py`](replay_T47_A_common.py).

- Established: the normal form in eight generators, with nothing discarded; the six faces where a coefficient vanishes, each reduced with the group to another contact case (T₂₉, T₃₆, T₁, T₃₉, T₄₂, T₇); the radial identity, with a degree-9 polynomial of 1,556 terms, as a full polynomial identity; the four-coefficient condition; and that the three sectors of the top-level note partition the positive quadrant.
- **Why T₄₇ has no verdict.** Part A uses, as statements it did not replay, the three sector notes (part B) and the notes for the face a = 0 (part C). Those parts are unfinished.
- Two remarks of part A that are not in the documents. The statements of the two necessity notes have an elementary proof that needs none of the three Appendix C results the notes cite. And the case is not vacuous: points of the stratum with all eight coefficients positive exist, but on the points found the four-coefficient condition holds, so the sector certificates were not exercised by actual points.
