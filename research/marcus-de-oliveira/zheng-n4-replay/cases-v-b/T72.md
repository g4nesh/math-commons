## Statement and scope

Case $T_{72}$: if $x\in X$ and $c(x)\in N_{T_{72}}$, then $\Phi_{c(x)}(O)\ge0$ for every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.

- **From $T'$ to $T$.** Let $g_0$ be the document's group element and $h=g_0^{-1}$. For every one of the 1152 group elements $g$ there is a permutation $\lambda_g$ of the eight labels with $p_\sigma(v\circ\lambda_g)=p_{g\sigma}(v)$ identically (EXACT, 24 polynomial identities per element). Hence $g$ maps the image of $\Psi$, and so $X$, onto itself. The matrix of $g_0$ on $V$ is an integer matrix with integer inverse, fixes $\kappa$ and permutes the 82 vectors $f_j$, and $\Phi_{g\cdot c}(g\cdot M)=\pm\Phi_c(M)$ for a generic $4\times4$ matrix with the sign of $\det(g\cdot M)/\det M$ (EXACT). So if $x\in X$ and $c(x)\in N_T$, then $h\cdot x\in X$, $c(h\cdot x)=h\cdot c(x)\in N_{T'}$, and the statement on $T'$ gives $\Phi_{c(x)}(O)=\Phi_{h\cdot c(x)}(O'')\ge0$, where $O\mapsto O''$ is a bijection of $\mathrm{SO}(4)$. PROOF-READ on top of the EXACT facts.
- **Non-strict contacts.** The argument uses only that the 24 gaps are $\ge0$ and vanish on $T'$. Zero coefficients, hence additional contacts, are allowed in every step.
- **Every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.** The conclusion is that $c(x)$ is a nonnegative combination of squared-minor vectors and of combinations covered by the cited inequalities, each of which holds on all of $\mathrm{SO}(4)$.
- **All spectra.** The proof never parametrizes by spectra. It uses only that $x\in X$, through $X\subset\{\nabla C=0\}$ (EXACT) and the two equations of PROOF.md 7.3. So repeated eigenvalues and $a_i+b_j=0$ are covered by the same argument, and nothing is delegated to Appendix B or Theorem C.1. PROOF-READ.
- **No use of the 82-facet theorem.** The eight vectors are a basis of the solution space of the six contact equations, seven coefficients are gaps, and the eighth is a difference of two gaps. The sign of that difference is treated in both cases. So Proposition 6.4 and Lemma 6.5 are not needed.

The logical shape: if $d\le0$ the gap vector is a nonnegative combination of eight squared-minor vectors. If $d>0$ (normalized to $d=1$), three sufficient conditions are available. At a point of $X$ the two radial equations force $G_9=0$ for an explicit polynomial $G_9$. The document shows $G_9>0$ at every point of the closed orthant at which all three conditions fail. Hence one of them holds.
