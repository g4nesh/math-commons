## The Gram certificates (step 4)

The PDF states four Gram matrices "in diagonal-coefficient PSD8 coordinates" and says that each satisfies ten relations. It does not state the representation. From the data I took: two blocks of eight cubic polynomials in the quaternion coordinates $y=(p,q)\in\mathbb R^8$, an invertible rational $8\times8$ matrix $T$, and four signed permutations $S_0,\dots,S_3$ of the coordinates. With $\varphi_{\rm blk}=T^{-1}F_{\rm blk}$ and a symmetric $8\times8$ matrix $\Gamma$ put $\Sigma(\Gamma)=\sum_j\sum_{\rm blk}\varphi_{\rm blk}(S_jy)^t\,\Gamma\,\varphi_{\rm blk}(S_jy)$.

**Derived here (EXACT).** With $M(p,q)$ the matrix of $x\mapsto pxq$ (checked: $M^tM=\lvert p\rvert^2\lvert q\rvert^2I$, $\det M=(\lvert p\rvert^2\lvert q\rvert^2)^2$) and the Leibniz sums of the eight generators rebuilt from the paper, normal forms modulo $\lvert p\rvert^2-\lvert q\rvert^2$ give 419 coefficient equations of rank 18. Their solutions are exactly: ten independent linear relations on $\Gamma$ (printed in the log), and

$$(\lvert p\rvert^2+\lvert q\rvert^2)\,\Sigma(\Gamma)\equiv4\,\Phi_{\sum_mw_mf_m}(M(p,q))\pmod{\lvert p\rvert^2-\lvert q\rvert^2},\qquad w=\operatorname{diag}\Gamma,$$

in the order $(f_0,f_1,f_2,f_{12},f_{59},f_{67},f_{70},f_{72})$. The ten relations stored in the supplement span the same space.

**Consequence (PROOF-READ + C6).** On unit quaternions $\Sigma(\Gamma)=2\Phi_w(M(p,q))$, so $\Gamma\succeq0$ gives $\Phi_w\ge0$ on $\mathrm{SO}(4)$. For each of the four matrices the script eliminates symmetrically, checks the pivots stated in step 4 and a zero residual, and evaluates the identity directly at one rational instance. The pivots are $\ge0$ exactly under the document's condition (the leaves being positive), so (2)–(5) hold on this face. A zero leaf makes the corresponding chain unavailable, as the document says.

The same data framework was verified by this owner for the paper's $T_{25}$ ([`../cases-v-a/T25.md`](../cases-v-a/T25.md)); the code is copied from there.
