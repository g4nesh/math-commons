## Statement and scope

Case $T_{39}$: if $x\in X$ and $c(x)\in N_{T_{39}}$, then $\Phi_{c(x)}(O)\ge0$ for every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.

- **Non-strict contacts.** The argument uses only $c(x)\in N_T$. Zero coefficients, hence additional contacts, are allowed throughout; the faces of the two eight-generator cones are treated explicitly (steps 13, 18, 27). PROOF-READ.
- **Every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.** The conclusion is a decomposition of $c(x)$ into squared-minor vectors and combinations covered by the cited inequalities, each valid on all of $\mathrm{SO}(4)$.
- **All spectra.** The proof never parametrizes by spectra. It uses only $x\in X$, through $X\subset\{\nabla C=0\}$ (EXACT) and the two equations of PROOF.md 7.3. Repeated eigenvalues and $a_i+b_j=0$ are covered by the same argument. Nothing is delegated to Appendix B or Theorem C.1. PROOF-READ.

There is no curve argument. The limits that occur are limits in the coefficient space (closures of chart domains in branch (1), an $\varepsilon$-perturbation of vanishing coefficients in branch (2)); their hypotheses are checked in steps 18 and 27.

The logical shape: $N_T$ has ten generators, two of them non-minor ($f_{40}$, $f_{70}$). Three linear identities reduce any vector of $N_T$ to a sum of minors, or to one of two forms with a single non-minor generator and eight independent generators. In each form the radial equations force a polynomial equation $G=0$. Branch (1): $G_{40}>0$ for $j$ below an endpoint $J$ that depends on the chamber of $(a,b,c,e,p)$, and $j\ge J$ is sufficient. Branch (2): $G_{70}>0$ whenever $as<1$ and $a<J(p,b,c,q)$, and each of $as\ge1$, $a\ge J$ is sufficient.
