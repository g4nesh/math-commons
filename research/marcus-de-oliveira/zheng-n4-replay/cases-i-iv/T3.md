## What the case says and how the document proves it

Statement (PROOF.md §8): if $x\in X$ has gap vector $g=Pc(x)\ge 0$ with $g=0$ on $T_3$, then $\Phi_g(O)\ge0$ for every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.

Notation of the document: $F_\sigma=\kappa\prod_i(A_i-C_{\sigma(i)})$ with $C=-B$, $g_\sigma=\operatorname{Re}(F_\sigma-F_0)$. This is the paper's $z_\sigma$ and $c(x)$ with $\pi_*=\pi_0$.

Outline. The cone $N_{T_3}$ has eleven generators, one of them not a minor vector ($f_{17}$). One linear functional $A$ decides membership in the minor cone. If $A<0$, the vector is written as minors plus $g_{22}f_1+g_6f_{11}+(-A)f_{17}$, and the document proves $A^2\le g_6g_{22}$ from the spectral identities. That is the coefficient condition of the SOC inequality for the triple $(f_1,f_{11};f_{17})$.
