**Transfer along the orbit (EXACT + PROOF-READ, own derivation).** For `g: σ ↦ ρσρ'` put `O'[a][b] = O[ρ'(a)][ρ^{-1}(b)]`. Then `w_{gσ}(O') = sgn(ρρ') w_σ(O)` for the Leibniz weights, so `Φ_{g·c}(O') = sgn(ρρ') Φ_c(O)`. The script verifies this as a polynomial identity in the 16 entries of `O` for the vectors of each tuple and checks that the minors of the used tuple are, up to sign and complementation, the minors of the representative at `O'`. If the sign is −1, changing the sign of one column of `O'` gives a matrix of SO(4) and restores the sign. So the pointwise inequality at the orbit member follows from the one at the representative.

**Pointwise form ⇒ coefficient form (PROOF-READ).** SOC: `a x_i^2 + b x_j^2 ≥ 2√(ab)|x_i x_j| ≥ 2d|x_i x_j|`. Four-coefficient: the matrix `[[a,−d,−d],[−d,b,−d],[−d,−d,c−d]]` has determinant `ab(c−d) − d^2(a+b+c+d)` (EXACT) and, under the condition with `d > 0`, nonnegative principal minors, so it is positive semidefinite and the quadratic form in `(|x_a|, |x_b|, |x_c|)` dominates `−d(Φ_d + ...)`.

## 4. Finding: the real-`v` stratum is trivial

On the chart every response `R_j` is a rational function of `(x, t, r, u, v)` with rational coefficients, and its denominator is a product of `t, v, v−1, v−x, Y, Q` (EXACT). These are nonzero for eight distinct points. So if `v` is real, all eight points are real, every `R_j` is real, and every gap `g_j = −μ Im R_j` is 0. The gap vector is the zero vector and the inequality is `0 ≥ 0`. The proof of T_10 therefore does not depend on the three-plus-three theorem. (NUMERIC confirmation: 200,000 chart points with real `v`, largest gap exactly 0.)
