## 5. The delegated sub-case can be closed directly (own argument, not in the document)

Suppose two eigenvalues of one spectrum coincide and the contact set contains $T_1$. If $A_i=A_j$ then $F_\sigma=F_{\sigma\circ(i\,j)}$ for every $\sigma$. If $C_i=C_j$ then $F_\sigma=F_{(i\,j)\circ\sigma}$ (EXACT, all 12 coincidences). So the gap vector $g$ and the contact set are invariant under the corresponding involution $s$ of the 24 indices, and the contact set contains $T'=T_1\cup s(T_1)$. Write $g=\sum_ja_jf_j$ with $a_j\ge0$ over the 13 generators of Section 3. A generator with $a_j>0$ must vanish on $T'$.

- For 11 of the 12 coincidences neither $f_{17}$ nor $f_{18}$ vanishes on $T'$ (EXACT). So $g$ is a nonnegative combination of minor vectors.
- The remaining coincidence is $A_2=A_3$, where $s(T_1)=T_1$. There $s$ permutes the 13 generators, maps minor vectors to minor vectors and exchanges $f_{17}$ and $f_{18}$ (EXACT). Since $g=s\cdot g$,
  $$g=\tfrac12(g+s\cdot g)=\sum_j\tfrac{a_j}{2}\,(f_j+s\cdot f_j),\qquad f_{17}+f_{18}=f_1+f_4+f_8+f_{15}+f_{16}\ \text{(EXACT)},$$
  so again $g$ is a nonnegative combination of minor vectors.

Hence $\Phi_{c(x)}\ge0$ in every repeated-eigenvalue sub-case, with no appeal to the one-circle theorem. Level: EXACT for the finite checks (block 9 of the script), PROOF-READ for the three-line argument. This argument is new in this replay and has not been reviewed by anyone else.

Which coincidences does the main branch itself exclude? Its six differences use only the pairs $A_0A_1$, $A_1A_2$, $A_1A_3$, $A_2A_3$ and the same four pairs of $C$ (block 3). The coincidences $A_0=A_2$, $A_0=A_3$, $C_0=C_2$, $C_0=C_3$ do not disturb it.
