## 5. The delegated sub-case can be closed directly (own argument, not in the document)

Suppose two eigenvalues of one spectrum coincide and the contact set contains $T_2$. If $A_i=A_j$ then $F_\sigma=F_{\sigma\circ(i\,j)}$; if $C_i=C_j$ then $F_\sigma=F_{(i\,j)\circ\sigma}$ (EXACT, all 12 coincidences). So the contact set is invariant under the corresponding involution $s$ and contains $T'=T_2\cup s(T_2)$. In $g=\sum_Ja_jf_j$ a generator with $a_j>0$ must vanish on $T'$.

For **all 12** coincidences $f_{17}$ does not vanish on $T'$ (EXACT; the sets $T'$ are printed in the log). So $g$ is a nonnegative combination of minor vectors, with no appeal to the one-circle theorem. For the two coincidences that the main branch cannot handle:

- $A_1=A_2$: $T'=\{0,1,2,3,4,5,6,8\}$ contains the entry face $\sigma(0)=0$.
- $C_1=C_2$: $T'=\{0,1,2,3,6,8,12,14\}$.

Level: EXACT for the finite checks (block 8), PROOF-READ for the argument. This argument is new in this replay and has not been reviewed by anyone else.
