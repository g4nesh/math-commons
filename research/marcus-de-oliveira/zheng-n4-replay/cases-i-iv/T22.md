## Statement and scope

Case $T_{22}$: if $x\in X$ and $c(x)\in N_{T_{22}}$, then $\Phi_{c(x)}(O)\ge 0$ for every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.

- **Non-strict contacts.** The argument uses only $c(x)\in N_T$. Additional contacts are allowed. PROOF-READ.
- **Every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.** On the layer the document treats itself the conclusion is a decomposition into minor vectors, valid for all $O$ at once (step 16). On the delegated layer the cited results hold for every $U\in\mathrm U(4)$.
- **All spectra.** Write $x=\alpha\Psi(v)$ (cited, C3).
  1. $\alpha=0$: $c(x)=0$. The document does not mention this sub-case; it is trivial.
  2. **Delegated.** The document sets aside every coincidence among the eight labels. Its argument needs only that eight specific brackets are nonzero (step 5). The delegated set is exactly: $a_1+b_1=0$, $a_2+b_3=0$, $a_3+b_2=0$, $a_4+b_4=0$ (paper Theorem B.1), or $a_1=a_3$, $a_2=a_4$, $b_1=b_2$, $b_3=b_4$ (paper Lemma B.2); indices 1-based. EXACT for the list, CITED for the two results.
  3. All other points: the document's argument. The document states it for eight distinct labels. It goes through unchanged when labels coincide outside that list (PROOF-READ: nothing else in it uses distinctness; supported by test 3 below). Under the document's own split those points are delegated as well, so every point is covered either way.

There is no curve, no limit and no perturbation in this proof. The Hessian equation is not used, only $C(u)=0$. No Gram matrix and no sum of squares occurs; the sign certificates are polynomials with positive coefficients.

## Falsification attempts

All in [`logs/replay_T22.txt`](logs/replay_T22.txt). Exact arithmetic unless marked NUMERIC.

1. **Sign certificates (steps 14, 15).** For each of the four normal forms, 10000 random rational points of $\{r\ge0,\ d>0\}$ (seeds 2254, 2256, 2269, 2270), with between none and almost all of the other coefficients set to 0 and $d$ ranging over fifteen orders of magnitude. $C$ evaluated directly in the 14 matching coordinates. $\sigma C\ge d^3$ every time; smallest ratio 1.
2. **Elimination (step 13).** 6000 random rational coefficient vectors, random order of steps, seed 2201.
3. **Stratum search, NUMERIC.** Unknowns $a,b\in\mathbb C^4$ and a phase; equations gaps $=0$ on $T$, gaps $\ge0$, sum of gaps $=1$; two-stage Levenberg–Marquardt from standard normal starts; a point counts when the maximal residual is below $10^{-10}$. 400 starts (seed 22001): 358 points. *Covered*: none of the eight named label distances below $10^{-3}$ (relative to the root mean square distance). *Delegated*: one of them below $10^{-4}$. Result: 90 covered, of which 36 have eight distinct labels; 268 delegated.
   - (a) $H=0$ at covered points: largest $\lvert H\rvert=1.3\cdot10^{-10}$. No violation at tolerance $10^{-7}$.
   - (b) gap vector in the cone of the nine minor generators (non-negative least squares): largest distance $4.7\cdot10^{-11}$. No violation.
   - (a″) every point with $\lvert H\rvert>10^{-6}$ is delegated: 172 such points in the free search and 269 more from 320 starts with $H$ forced to $\pm0.3,\pm1$ (seeds 22101–22104); largest value of the smallest named distance $8.2\cdot10^{-9}$. No violation.
4. **NUMERIC evidence for C1 and C2.** For the paper's $O_*$, 60 random matrices in $\mathrm{SO}(4)$ and 60 Haar unitary matrices (seed 22900), a linear programme finds common weights for $a_1+b_1=0$, for $b_3=b_4$ and for $a_3=a_4$; the weights reproduce the determinant at three random spectra each (residual $\le2.2\cdot10^{-15}$). Without a coincidence the same programme is infeasible at $O_*$ and at 10 of the 120 random matrices. Evidence for the cited statements, not a proof.

## Observations

- **Only eight coincidences are delegated.** The document asks for eight distinct labels; its argument needs only the eight brackets of step 5. Of the 90 covered points of test 3, 54 have coincident labels outside the named eight.
- **A seventh contact is forced on the layer of eight distinct labels.** $p_2p_{13}=p_3p_{12}$ identically (EXACT), and $2,3,12\in T$. With $H=0$ and nonzero products, $\alpha z_{13}=(\alpha z_3)(\alpha z_{12})/(\alpha z_2)$ is purely imaginary, so 13 is a contact. Hence no point with eight distinct labels has exactly the six contacts $T_{22}$; the closure of $T_{22}$ under the 18 relations of this kind is $T_{22}\cup\{13\}$. All 36 points with eight distinct labels found in test 3 have contact set $\{2,3,4,8,10,12,13\}$. This is not in the document and not needed by it; the proof covers additional contacts.
- In the numeric search the gap vector lay in the minor cone at every point found, delegated ones included (largest distance $1.3\cdot10^{-10}$).
