## Observations not in the document

**O1. For eight distinct labels, all gaps $\ge0$ and real $v$, the gap vector is zero** (EXACT identity, PROOF-READ deduction; script Section 6b).

1. $x,h,k,l$ are real for every configuration with eight distinct labels. The parameter $q$ may be nonreal while $v$ is real, because (2) is a real quadratic in $w$ for real $v$.
2. $\operatorname{Im}v/\operatorname{Im}q=(h/\lvert q\rvert^2)(1+g_4/g_3)$ identically. If $q$ is nonreal, then $g_3\ne0$ and $g_4\ne0$ by (4), so both are positive under support, and $\operatorname{Im}v\ne0$.
3. So real $v$ forces real $q$. Then $w$, $a_2$, $A_3$ are real, every $R_j$ is real and all gaps vanish. Checked at two rational points.

Unlike for $T_{18}$ and $T_{19}$, this uses the hypothesis that the gaps are nonnegative. The case then depends on Appendix B for collisions only.
