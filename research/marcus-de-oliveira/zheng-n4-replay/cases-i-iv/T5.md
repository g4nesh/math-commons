## What the case says and how the documents prove it

Statement: if $x\in X$ has gap vector $g\ge0$ with $g=0$ on $T_5$, then $\Phi_g(O)\ge0$ on $\mathrm{SO}(4)$.

Notation: $F_\pi=\kappa\prod_i(A_i-C_{\pi(i)})$, $C=-B$, $g_\pi=\operatorname{Re}(F_\pi-F_1)$. The reference permutation is $\pi_1\in T_5$.

Outline. $N_{T_5}$ has eleven generators, one non-minor ($f_{54}$). With $A_0=g_{16}-g_0$: if $A_0\ge0$ three linear identities give a minor decomposition. - (4) $d^2\le g_{10}g_{13}$: SOC for $(f_5,f_2;f_{54})$,
- (5) $g_{22}g_{17}(g_7-d)\ge d^2(g_{22}+g_{17}+g_7+d)$: four-coefficient for $(f_0,f_6;f_{10};f_{54})$.

In a chart with real parameters $x,t,r,u$ the document shows that (4) holds when $x<0$ and when $x>1$, $H<0$, and that (5) holds when $x>1$, $H>0$. Each time the inequality is a concave quadratic in $d$ that is positive at $d=0$ and nonnegative at the other end of the interval allowed by the gaps.
