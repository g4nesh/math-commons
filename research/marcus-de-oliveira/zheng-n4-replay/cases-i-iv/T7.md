Polarizing in $r$ gives $g_6=-\mu\operatorname{Im}r\,s/\lvert r\rvert^2$, $g_{12}=-(v-1)g_6$, $g_{18}=\mu\operatorname{Im}r\,hsv/\lvert r\rvert^2$, $g_{10}(v-1)=g_{18}$. The four formulas were also confirmed at three exact points with Gaussian-rational $r$, directly from the 24 products.

Consequence: for eight distinct labels either $v$ is nonreal, where the document's argument gives a minor decomposition, or $c(x)=0$. Theorem C.1 is then needed only for a repeated label, where Lemma B.2 also applies. The audit's dependency summary lists $T_7$ among the cases that need Theorem C.1 in full; this replay does not confirm that need.
