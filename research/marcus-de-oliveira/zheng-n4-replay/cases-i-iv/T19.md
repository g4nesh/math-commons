## Observations not in the document

**O1. The supported layer with eight distinct labels and nonreal $v$ is empty** (EXACT identities and certificates, script Section 5b; PROOF-READ deduction).

1. On the chart, $g_{17}=h\,g_{15}$ and $g_{22}=-g_{19}/(k-1)$ identically.
2. The four gaps $g_{15},g_{17},g_{19},g_{22}$ have a common numerator $N$: with $N:=(g_{15}/g_2)\,k\,\lvert v-1\rvert^2\lvert D\rvert^2$, a polynomial, $g_{19}/g_2=-(h-1)(k-1)N/(k^2\lvert v-1\rvert^2\lvert D\rvert^2)$ and $g_{22}/g_2=(h-1)N/(k^2\lvert v-1\rvert^2\lvert D\rvert^2)$.
3. $N=\alpha\lvert v\rvert^2+\beta(v+\bar v)+\gamma$ with $\alpha,\beta,\gamma\in\mathbb Q[x,h,k,l]$ and $\alpha\gamma-\beta^2=-hk^2(k-1)(l-1)(x-1)PL$.
4. In region I: $\alpha>0$ and $\alpha\gamma-\beta^2>0$ (certificates), so $N=\alpha\lvert v+\beta/\alpha\rvert^2+(\alpha\gamma-\beta^2)/\alpha>0$ for every complex $v$. Since $(h-1)/k^2<0$, $g_{22}/g_2<0$. With $g_2>0$ (step 8) this gives $g_{22}<0$.
5. In region II: $\alpha<0$ and $\alpha\gamma-\beta^2>0$, so $N<0$ for every $v$. Since $g_{17}/g_2=hN/(k\lvert v-1\rvert^2\lvert D\rvert^2)$ and $h/k>0$, $g_{17}<0$.
6. By step 9 every supported configuration of the layer lies in region I or II. So there is none.

Numerical confirmation: 8,000 exact samples in the two regions all have the stated negative gap; 100,000 unrestricted chart samples contain no supported point in exact arithmetic; a Nelder–Mead maximization of the smallest off-contact gap over both regions stays at 0 to rounding (test N4).

**O2. For eight distinct labels and real $v$ the gap vector is zero** (PROOF-READ, EXACT spot check; script Section 6b). $x,h,k,l$ are real for every configuration with eight distinct labels; $a_2$, $w$, $A_3$ are rational functions of $(x,h,k,l,v)$ with rational coefficients; so real $v$ makes all labels and all $R_j$ real, and $g_j=-\mu\operatorname{Im}R_j=0$.
