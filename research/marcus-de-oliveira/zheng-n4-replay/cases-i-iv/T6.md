## What the case says and how the document proves it

Statement: if $x\in X$ has gap vector $g\ge0$ with $g=0$ on $T_6$, then $\Phi_g(O)\ge0$ on $\mathrm{SO}(4)$.

Notation: $F_\sigma=\lambda\prod_i(A_i-C_{\sigma(i)})$, $C=-B$, $g_\sigma=\operatorname{Re}(F_\sigma-F_0)$.

Outline. $N_{T_6}$ has thirteen generators, two non-minor ($f_{22}$, $f_{25}$). Two linear functionals $A$, $B$ decide membership in the minor cone. Cross-collisions are settled by explicit minor decompositions. For eight distinct points the four contact ratios put four quadruples of points on circles, which gives a chart with real parameters $r,s,h,t$ and a complex $v$. The document shows $A,B\ge0$ except in two regions (Case I with $0<r<1$, $h<0$; Case II with $\beta<0$). There it subtracts two entry vectors and writes the remainder as an explicit combination of $f_1,f_3,f_5,f_9,f_{10},f_{22},f_{23}$ whose coefficients on $(f_1,f_9;f_{22})$ satisfy the SOC condition. A relabelling $A_1\leftrightarrow A_2$, which preserves $T_6$, is used to normalise signs.
