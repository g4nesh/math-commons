## 4. Remark: how much of Theorem C.1 is used

On the chart every `R_j` is a rational function of `(h, t, s, l, v)` with rational coefficients whose denominator is a product of `h, h−1, t, v−1, M_1(v), M_2(v)` (EXACT). The identity between the responses of a general configuration and these functions holds as an identity of rational functions on the space of configurations (step 6). So for eight distinct points with real `v` and `M_1(v)M_2(v) ≠ 0`, all `R_j` are real and the gap vector is zero. Theorem C.1 is therefore needed at most for real `v` with `M_1(v)M_2(v) = 0`, a set of real codimension two. I did not analyse that set further; there the citation is used as stated. (NUMERIC: 200,000 chart points with real `v`, largest gap exactly 0.) The first sentence is EXACT, the conclusion PROOF-READ.
