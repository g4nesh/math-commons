## Statement and scope

Case $T_{21}$: if $x\in X$ and $c(x)\in N_{T_{21}}$, then $\Phi_{c(x)}(O)\ge 0$ for every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.

How the document covers it, and what I checked about the scope:

- **Non-strict contacts.** The argument uses only $c(x)\in N_T$: gaps $\ge0$ everywhere and $=0$ on $T$. Additional contacts are allowed. PROOF-READ.
- **Every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.** On the layer the document treats itself, the conclusion is that $c(x)$ is a nonnegative combination of minor vectors, which gives $\Phi\ge0$ for all $O$ at once (step 17). On the delegated layer the cited results hold for every $U\in\mathrm U(4)$.
- **All spectra.** Write $x=\alpha\Psi(v)$ (cited, see C3). Three sub-cases.
  1. $\alpha=0$: $c(x)=0$. Trivial.
  2. **Delegated.** The document sets aside every coincidence among the eight labels. Its argument in fact needs only that eight specific brackets are nonzero (step 6). So the delegated set is exactly: $a_1+b_1=0$, $a_2+b_3=0$, $a_3+b_4=0$, $a_4+b_2=0$ (paper Theorem B.1), or $a_1=a_4$, $a_2=a_3$, $b_1=b_2$, $b_3=b_4$ (paper Lemma B.2); indices 1-based. EXACT for the list, CITED for the two results.
  3. All other points: the document's argument. The document states it for eight distinct labels. It goes through unchanged when labels coincide outside that list (PROOF-READ: nothing else in it uses distinctness; supported by test 3 below). Under the document's own split those points are delegated as well, so every point is covered either way.

There is no curve, no limit and no perturbation in this proof. The Hessian equation $\operatorname{Hess}C(u)v=0$ is not used, only $C(u)=0$. No Gram matrix and no sum of squares occurs; the sign certificate is a polynomial with positive coefficients.

## Falsification attempts

All in [`logs/replay_T21.txt`](logs/replay_T21.txt). Exact arithmetic unless marked NUMERIC.

1. **Sign certificate (step 16).** 20000 random rational points of $\{r\ge0,\ d>0\}$, seed 2103, with between none and almost all of the other seven coefficients set to 0 and $d$ ranging over fifteen orders of magnitude. $C$ evaluated directly in the 14 matching coordinates, not through the expanded polynomial. $C\ge d^3$ every time; smallest ratio $C/d^3=1$.
2. **Elimination (step 14).** 8000 random rational coefficient vectors, seeds 2101 and 2102.
3. **Stratum search, NUMERIC.** Unknowns $a,b\in\mathbb C^4$ and a phase; equations gaps $=0$ on $T$, gaps $\ge0$, sum of gaps $=1$; two-stage Levenberg–Marquardt from standard normal starts; a point counts when the maximal residual is below $10^{-10}$. 1500 starts (seeds 21001–21003): 1364 points. Call a point *covered* if none of the eight named label distances is below $10^{-3}$ (relative to the root mean square distance) and *delegated* if one is below $10^{-4}$. Result: 405 covered, 957 delegated, 2 in between.
   - (a) $H=0$ at covered points: largest $\lvert H\rvert=5.9\cdot10^{-10}$. No violation at tolerance $10^{-7}$.
   - (b) gap vector in the cone of the nine minor generators (non-negative least squares): largest distance $2.8\cdot10^{-10}$. No violation.
   - (c) $\delta\le0$ at covered points: $\delta\in[-0.25,\ 1.6\cdot10^{-10}]$. No violation.
   - (a″) every point with $\lvert H\rvert>10^{-6}$ is delegated: 694 such points in the free search and 273 more from 320 starts with $H$ forced to $\pm0.3,\pm1$ (seeds 21101–21104); the largest value of the smallest named distance is $3.7\cdot10^{-8}$. No violation.
4. **NUMERIC evidence for C1 and C2.** For the paper's $O_*$, 60 random matrices in $\mathrm{SO}(4)$ and 60 Haar unitary matrices (seed 21900), a linear programme finds common weights for $a_1+b_1=0$, for $b_3=b_4$ and for $a_3=a_4$; the weights reproduce the determinant at three random spectra each (residual $\le2\cdot10^{-15}$). Without a coincidence the same programme is infeasible at $O_*$ and at 7 of the 120 random matrices. This is evidence for the cited statements, not a proof of them. A first run with the solver's default feasibility tolerance gave one residual of $1.2\cdot10^{-9}$ against my threshold $10^{-9}$; with the tolerance set to $10^{-10}$ it is $1.8\cdot10^{-15}$. It was a solver artefact.

## Observations

- **Only eight coincidences are delegated.** The document asks for eight distinct labels. Its argument needs only the eight brackets of step 6. Test 3 confirms this numerically on 405 points that have coincident labels but none of the eight named ones.
- **No stratum point with eight distinct labels was found.** All 1364 points have two coincident labels. A second search that keeps all 28 label distances above 0.05 or 0.01 never reached a zero residual in 160 starts (best $10^{-6}$), while the same search on $T_{22}$ succeeds in 11 of 160. I did not decide whether that layer is empty. It is not needed by the proof. If it is empty, the content of this case is carried by step 4 and by the coincidences outside the named eight.
- In the numeric search the gap vector lay in the minor cone at every point found, delegated ones included (largest $\delta$: $1.6\cdot10^{-10}$).
- $T_{21}$ is closed under completing any of the 18 relations $p_ap_d=p_bp_c$ that have three corners in the set (EXACT), so no seventh contact is forced in the way it is for $T_{22}$ and $T_{23}$.
- The polynomial of step 15 is the document's "$P_{33}$" for $T_{23}$ after renaming the variables.
