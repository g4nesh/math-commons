## The argument in outline

$N_T$ has eight generators of rank 8, so $g=c(x)=a f_0+e f_1+p f_2+j f_5+b f_6+c f_{39}+q f_{67}+d f_{70}$ with unique coefficients $\ge0$; only $f_{70}=h_{4231,+}$ is not a minor vector. For $d>0$ set $d=1$.

1. **Radial equation.** $C(g+h\kappa)=-C_*+hQ$, $C_*>0$; then $Q^4\det\operatorname{Hess}C=-4Q^2G^2$ with $G$ of degree 9, quadratic in $p$. So $G=0$.
2. **Capacities**, each of the form "$p\ge P_*$": SOC $pj\ge1$; old-H $c(p-1)\ge1$; (6), (7), double SOC; (9), (10) of Lemma 1. Two more do not involve $p$: the four-coefficient inequalities (5). Assume both fail strictly.
3. **Exclusion.** $k_0=G\vert_{p=0}=C_*G_6>0$. On twenty baseline charts and four new ones the two further Bernstein coefficients of $G$ on $[0,P_*]$ are $\ge0$. So a zero of $G$ has $p\ge P_*$. The corner $j=c=0$, where no threshold exists, has $G>0$.
