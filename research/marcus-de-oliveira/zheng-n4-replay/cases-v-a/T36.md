## Statement and scope

Case $T_{36}$: if $x\in X$ and $c(x)\in N_{T_{36}}$, then $\Phi_{c(x)}(O)\ge0$ for every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.

- **Non-strict contacts.** Only $c(x)\in N_T$ is used. PROOF-READ.
- **Every $O$.** Each branch ends with a nonnegative combination of minor vectors plus one cited combination with $\Phi\ge0$ on $\mathrm{SO}(4)$, or with a contradiction.
- **All spectra.** Only $x\in X$ is used, through $\nabla C=0$ on $X$. Repeated eigenvalues and $a_i+b_j=0$ are covered by the same argument; nothing is delegated.
- **No curve.** The only limits are limits of polynomial inequalities in the coefficients (step 17).

## Observations

- Both hard branches are realized by points of $X$ (numerically), with small $d$; all points found are safe by the simplest condition of their branch.
- In (69) the hard set is defined by the failure of all four conditions (4), and $C_*>0$ is proved only there (step 6). This is enough: outside the hard set the point is safe without any use of $G_{69}$.
- The document's term counts are reproduced when each variable's reduced denominator is cleared to the degree of $G$ in that variable.
- In chamber 00 the correction (7) is needed for exactly three monomials; I confirmed that these are the only negative coefficients of $Z-MH^2$.
