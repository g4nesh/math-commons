## Statement and scope

Case $T_{27}$: if $x\in X$ and $c(x)\in N_{T_{27}}$, then $\Phi_{c(x)}(O)\ge0$ for every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.

- **Non-strict contacts.** The argument uses only $c(x)\in N_T$, that is, gaps $\ge0$ and gaps $=0$ on $T$. Further contacts are allowed. PROOF-READ.
- **Every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.** The conclusion is a decomposition of $c(x)$ into minor vectors plus one group of three or four vectors to which a cited inequality applies; both hold at every $O$.
- **All spectra.** The only property of $x$ that is used is $\nabla C(x)=0$, which holds on all of $X$: it is a polynomial identity on the image of $\Psi$ (EXACT, 14 identities in 16 variables), and $X$ is the closure of that image. No distinctness of eigenvalues, no finite chart and no non-vanishing of any $a_i+b_j$ enters. So repeated eigenvalues and $a_i+b_j=0$ are covered by the document's own argument, and nothing is delegated to Appendix B or Theorem C.1. If $c(x)=0$ the statement is trivial.

There is no curve, no limit and no perturbation in this proof. All sign statements are on closed regions and involve no division.

## Falsification attempts

All in [`logs/replay_T27.txt`](logs/replay_T27.txt).

1. **Sign of $G_6$ (step 13), exact arithmetic.** 20000 random rational points of $\{pj<d^2,\ K<0,\ d>0\}$ (seed 2704), with up to 70 per cent of the other coefficients set to 0 and values spread over eight orders of magnitude: $G_6>0$ every time, smallest $G_6/d^6=0.72$. The statement is not vacuous: among 20000 random rational points of the whole orthant (seed 2705), $G_6\le0$ occurs 963 times, each time with $pj\ge d^2$ or $K\ge0$.
2. **Normalisation (step 6).** 4000 random coefficient vectors, seed 2702.
3. **The two cited inequalities on their boundaries, NUMERIC.** Minimum of the Leibniz sum over $\mathrm{SO}(4)$ (BFGS from 16 random starts in the quaternion model, seed 27001). SOC with $pj=d^2$: 12 values of $p/d\in[e^{-3},e^{3}]$, smallest value $7.7\cdot10^{-19}$. Four-coefficient with $K=0$: 12 random $(a,b)$ with $ab>d^2$, smallest value $3.6\cdot10^{-18}$. Tolerance $-10^{-9}$; no violation. Both tests detect a violation when the condition is dropped ($-0.0049$ at $pj=d^2/4$; $-0.0026$ at $(a,b,e,d)=(2,2,1,1)$).
4. **Points of the stratum, NUMERIC.** Unknowns $a,b\in\mathbb C^4$ and a phase; equations gaps $=0$ on $T$, gaps $\ge0$, sum of gaps $=1$; two-stage Levenberg–Marquardt from standard normal starts; a point counts when the maximal residual is below $10^{-10}$. 1500 starts (seeds 27300–27302): 1336 points; the gap vector lies in the cone of the ten generators at each (largest distance $2.8\cdot10^{-10}$). After the normalisation 1314 are minor combinations and 22 reach the normal form (4) with $d>0$. At these 22 points, scaled to $d=1$: $\lvert G_6\rvert\le2.0\cdot10^{-12}$ times the sum of the absolute values of its 401 terms (the radial equation of step 11), and Lemma 1 holds with $pj\ge d^2$ at all 22 (smallest value of $\max(pj-d^2,K)$: $1.7$); $K\ge0$ holds as well at one of them.
