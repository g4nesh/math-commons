## Statement and scope

Case $T_{28}$: if $x\in X$ and $c(x)\in N_{T_{28}}$, then $\Phi_{c(x)}(O)\ge0$ for every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.

- **Non-strict contacts.** Only $c(x)\in N_T$ is used; further contacts are allowed. PROOF-READ.
- **Every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.** The conclusion is a decomposition of $c(x)$ into minor vectors and one or two groups to which a cited inequality applies; all hold at every $O$.
- **All spectra.** The only property of $x$ used is $\nabla C(x)=0$, a polynomial identity on the image of $\Psi$ (EXACT) and hence on its closure $X$. No distinctness of eigenvalues and no non-vanishing of any $a_i+b_j$ enters. Nothing is delegated to Appendix B or Theorem C.1. If $c(x)=0$ the statement is trivial.

There is no curve argument. The limits that occur are limits in the coefficient space of the normal form (closures of chart domains); their hypotheses are checked in step 21.
