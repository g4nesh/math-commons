## Statement and scope

Case $T_{31}$: if $x\in X$ and $c(x)\in N_{T_{31}}$, then $\Phi_{c(x)}(O)\ge0$ for every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.

- **Non-strict contacts.** The argument uses only $c(x)\in N_T$. Zero coefficients, hence additional contacts, are allowed in every step (steps 20–24 treat the faces of the cone explicitly). PROOF-READ.
- **Every $O\in\mathrm{SO}(4)$.** The conclusion is that $c(x)$ is a nonnegative combination of squared-minor vectors and of combinations covered by the cited inequalities, each of which holds on all of $\mathrm{SO}(4)$.
- **All spectra.** The proof never parametrizes by spectra. It uses only that $x\in X$, through $X\subset\{\nabla C=0\}$ (EXACT) and the two equations of PROOF.md 7.3. So repeated eigenvalues and $a_i+b_j=0$ are covered by the same argument, and nothing is delegated to Appendix B or Theorem C.1. PROOF-READ.

The logical shape: for $d>0$ (normalized to $d=1$) the two radial equations force a polynomial equation $G_9=0$ in the seven remaining coefficients. The document shows that $G_9>0$ at every point of the closed orthant at which none of four sufficient conditions holds. Hence one of them holds, and each gives $\Phi\ge0$.
