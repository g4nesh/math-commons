# Independent replay of the contact cases of group V, first half (Zheng's proof for n = 4)

- **Task:** `mdo-zheng-cases-v-a`, requested by the audit `mdo-zheng-n4-audit` (owner `IshaanAyaan`). Replay by `ganesh-claude-minors` (Claude Code, model `claude-fable-5-2`) and its workers, GitHub owner `g4nesh`. November 26, 2026.
- **What this is.** The same procedure as [`../cases-i-iv/`](../cases-i-iv/README.md): for each case one worker read the proof document in the author's public supplement and replayed every identity and certificate with its own exact code, without opening the audit's first-pass report on the same document until its verdict was written. The supplement's programs were never executed. Nothing from the supplement is copied here; scripts read it from a local clone named by the environment variable `MOC4_SUPP`.
- **Group V.** The real part of a point of the matching cone satisfies C = 0 and a Hessian equation. Written in the generators of the contact cone, these exclude, by polynomial sign certificates, the region where every universal inequality on SO(4) fails.
- **Status.** All ten cases of the task are reported. A verdict here is one independent reading by a second owner, not a formal verification. The coordinator re-ran every replay script before committing it.
- **Evidence levels and verdict words** are those of [`../../zheng-n4-audit/METHOD.md`](../../zheng-n4-audit/METHOD.md).

## Summary of the batch

- **All ten cases: confirmed modulo citations. No gap was found**, and there is no mathematical disagreement with the audit's reports on these ten documents. For T₃₄ both readings find the same unproved step, the sufficiency of condition (H), which is the old-H inequality. The ten replay scripts run 990 checks in total (868 exact, 112 numeric, 10 comparisons with the supplement's data); the coordinator re-ran each script and each exits 0.
- **What the ten cases rest on.** None delegates anything to Appendix B, to Theorem C.1, to a seven-contact set or to another contact case as a case. Every proof ends in inequalities on SO(4) that involve no spectra:
  - the four families of [`../../zheng-n4-audit/PROOF.md`](../../zheng-n4-audit/PROOF.md), Section 7.6: old-H, chain, four-coefficient and SOC. Each use is pinned to the family's representative by an explicit group element, exactly;
  - Lemma 1 of the document for T₂₅ (coherent, parallel and double-SOC bounds), used by T₂₈ and T₃₇ and verified exactly in [T25.md](T25.md). T₂₆ does **not** need it (its bounds are re-proved on its own face), so the dependence of T₂₆ on T₂₅ recorded in PROOF.md disappears.
- **Where the cited inequalities are verified in this repository.** Old-H is inequality (★), Theorem O of [`../../star-orthogonal/README.md`](../../star-orthogonal/README.md), and is also proved in the audit's notes; [T34.md](T34.md) replays it. The chain inequality is verified exactly from the supplement's certificate data in [T34.md](T34.md); no PDF of the supplement contains a proof. The identities behind the SOC and four-coefficient inequalities are replayed in [`../cases-i-iv/`](../cases-i-iv/README.md) (the four-coefficient ones in [`replay_T3_common.py`](../cases-i-iv/replay_T3_common.py)); their proofs are those of the audit's [`foundations/`](../../zheng-n4-audit/foundations/). None of these has been reviewed by a third owner.
- **Links that are now closed.** In every case the factorization of Q⁴ · det Hess C on the lift, which produces the polynomial of the exclusion, is a full polynomial identity computed from the definitions. For eight of the ten documents the audit's first pass had checked it at sample points only. Every table size and every count of exceptional groups stated in the documents is reproduced.
- **Things the documents leave out** (none affects a conclusion): the two endpoint inequalities behind condition (H) of T₃₄ (they are old-H); the Gram relations behind Lemma 1 of T₂₅ and behind the bounds of T₂₆, which exist only as data in the supplement; a data file of binomial squares named by the T₂₆ document and absent from the public supplement (the squares were found here); a strict inequality in T₃₆ that holds only non-strictly on a boundary; a statement in T₃₇ written for O(4) that holds on SO(4) only; a loosely written display in T₂₉.
- **What this does not show.** The verdicts are one independent reading by a second owner. The proofs of the cited inequalities are outside these ten cases. So is Theorem 1.2 of the paper (the squared minors of a unitary 4×4 matrix are a convex combination of those of two real orthogonal matrices), for which [`../../unitary-factorization/README.md`](../../unitary-factorization/README.md) has an independent proof (Corollary F1). The reports enumerate the extreme rays of each contact cone directly, so they do not use the completeness of the paper's list of 82 facets. Group V has ten more cases and group VI has 54; they are not replayed here.

## Reproduction

Python 3.9 or later with sympy 1.14 and python-flint 0.6 (exact replays), numpy and scipy (falsification attempts only). From this directory:

```sh
export MOC4_SUPP=/path/to/clone/of/the/supplement   # commit 53ec16567d2307266ce52a8e9678410b2457cc27
for k in 25 26 27 28 29 31 34 35 36 37; do python3 replay_T$k.py > /dev/null; echo "T$k exit $?"; done   # about 3 minutes each
```

Each script prints its checks and a final `PASS` or `FAIL` line and exits 0 only if every check passes. The outputs of the committed runs are in [`logs/`](logs/). Without `MOC4_SUPP` the comparisons with the supplement's data are skipped where a script says so; the scripts that read a certificate from the supplement (T₂₅, T₂₆, T₃₄) need it.
