## The argument in outline

Write $g=c(x)$. The cone $N_T$ has ten generators; two are not minor vectors, $f_{54}=h_{4231,-}$ and $f_{70}=h_{4231,+}$.

1. **Canonical form.** Either $g$ is a nonnegative combination of minor vectors, or, after possibly replacing $x$ by its image under one element of $G$ that fixes $T$, $g=q f_0+p f_2+j f_{59}+c f_6+e f_{53}+d f_{54}+b f_{55}+a f_{67}$ with $d>0$ and the rest $\ge0$.
2. **Radial equation.** $C(g+h\kappa)=-C_*+hQ$ with $C_*\ge d^3>0$, so $h=C_*/Q$; then $Q^4\det\operatorname{Hess}C(g+h\kappa)=-4Q^2G^2$ for a polynomial $G$ of degree 9. Radial necessity (PROOF.md 7.3) gives $G=0$.
3. **Capacities.** Six sufficient conditions for $\Phi_g\ge0$ on $\mathrm{SO}(4)$, each of the form $j\ge J_*$(other coefficients): SOC, old-H, four-coefficient (cited), and (5), (6), double-SOC (Lemma 1).
4. **Exclusion.** With $d=1$, $G=k_2j^2+k_1j+k_0$. On seven charts covering the closed coefficient domain, $k_0>0$ and the two further Bernstein coefficients of $G$ on $[0,J_*]$ are $\ge0$. So $G>0$ for $j<J_*$, and a zero of $G$ has $j\ge J_*$, where a capacity applies.

## The Gram representation (steps 13–16)

The PDF does not state the representation it uses. From the data I took: two blocks of eight cubic polynomials in the quaternion coordinates $y=(p,q)\in\mathbb R^8$, an invertible rational $8\times8$ matrix $T$, and four signed permutations $S_0,\dots,S_3$ of the coordinates (each permutes $p$ and $q$ separately). With $\varphi_{\rm blk}=T^{-1}F_{\rm blk}$ put, for a symmetric $8\times8$ matrix $\Gamma$,

$$\Sigma(\Gamma)=\sum_{j=0}^{3}\sum_{{\rm blk}}\varphi_{\rm blk}(S_jy)^t\,\Gamma\,\varphi_{\rm blk}(S_jy).$$

Rebuilt here from the definitions: $M(p,q)$, the matrix of $x\mapsto pxq$ ($M^tM=\lvert p\rvert^2\lvert q\rvert^2I$, $\det M=(\lvert p\rvert^2\lvert q\rvert^2)^2$, exact), and the Leibniz sums $\Phi_{f_m}(M(p,q))$ of the eight generators of (1).

**Derived here (EXACT).** Normal forms modulo $N=\lvert p\rvert^2-\lvert q\rvert^2$ ($p_0^2$ eliminated; 418 coefficient equations of rank 18 in the 36 entries of $\Gamma$ and the 8 weights): the pairs $(\Gamma,w)$ with

$$(\lvert p\rvert^2+\lvert q\rvert^2)\,\Sigma(\Gamma)\equiv4\,\Phi_{\sum_mw_mf_m}(M(p,q))\pmod N\tag{$*$}$$

are exactly those with ten independent linear relations on $\Gamma$ (printed in the log) and $w=(\Gamma_{00},\Gamma_{11},\Gamma_{22},\Gamma_{33},\Gamma_{44},\Gamma_{55}-\Gamma_{00},\Gamma_{66},\Gamma_{77})$, in the order $(f_0,f_2,f_{59},f_6,f_{53},f_{54},f_{55},f_{67})$. This is the document's diagonal $(q,p,j,c,e,q+d,b,a)$. The ten relations stored in the supplement span the same space as mine.

**Consequence (PROOF-READ + C4).** On unit quaternions $(*)$ reads $\Sigma(\Gamma)=2\Phi_w(M(p,q))$. If $\Gamma\succeq0$ then $\Sigma(\Gamma)\ge0$. Every element of $\mathrm{SO}(4)$ is $M(p,q)$ for unit $p,q$ (classical). So $\Phi_w\ge0$ on $\mathrm{SO}(4)$.

**Positive semidefiniteness (EXACT).** For each construction the script eliminates symmetrically along a stated order and checks that the pivots are the stated expressions and that the residual matrix is zero, so $\Gamma=\sum_i\mathrm{col}_i\mathrm{col}_i^t/\mathrm{pivot}_i$. The pivots are visibly $\ge0$ under the hypotheses. Each construction is also checked by evaluating $(*)$ directly at one rational instance, without the linear algebra.

**What the representation does not give (EXACT).** With $q=p=c=a=b=0$ the relations force $J\!\cdot\!E=-1$, $J\!\cdot\!D=-1$ and $J\!\cdot\!D=0$ at once: the ten relations have no solution supported on $(J,E,D)$ with $\Gamma_{55}=1$ (rank check in the script). So the old-H capacity $e(j-d)\ge d^2$ has no certificate of this form when only $e,j,d$ are present. It is a genuine citation.
