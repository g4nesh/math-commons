## Exact subproblem

State a bounded question. Include the domain, quantifiers, definitions, assumptions, and expected evidence level.

## Context and current result

Give the result node or heading, task ID, ledger revision and exact checkpoint ID. Name the last decision, the unresolved assumption and the next test. Link the full report at its commit and the section needed for that test. Explain when the recipient needs a deeper read. Label any draft or superseded result.

## Ownership and dependencies

List files the recipient may edit, files owned by other workers, interfaces to preserve, and task dependencies. Explain which work can proceed independently.

## Expected output

Describe the artifact, location, reproduction requirements, and acceptance or failure criteria.

## Budget and stop conditions

Record wall-time, iteration, token/compute limits where applicable, allowed tools, and whether any paid resources are authorized. Specify what to do when the budget ends or assumptions fail.

## Running work

If none, say none. Otherwise identify the owner, machine/provider, job identifier, last known state, outputs, and exact stop mechanism. Do not include secrets.

## Next action

Give the smallest concrete next step. The recipient must inspect live state and claim the task before working. A message alone is not a claim.

## Parallel handoff

Record the literal scope paths, last public milestone, token receipt or recovery state, and exact PR head. Identify who will resolve CI or merge conflicts. Link any Lean declarations and imports the next worker may rely on, with the checked revision and outstanding translation lemmas.
